Distance warning for a visually impaired person

Proposal

A device attached to a cane or clothing that warns with vibration of an obstacle at head height.

Intermediateabout 8 lessonsAhjo 2

Problem
A white cane finds obstacles on the ground but not those at head height, such as an open window or a sign.
You learn
distance measurement with an ultrasonic sensor, controlling a vibration motor, interviewing a user, 3D modelling a mount
Materials
Arduino Uno, ultrasonic distance sensor, vibration motor or buzzer, battery holder, 3D-printed case
Measure of success
The device warns of an obstacle at head height from a distance of 1.0–1.5 metres at least 9 times out of 10, and false alarms are recorded.
Further development
The strength of the vibration changes with the distance. The device is tested with the target group as agreed with the teacher.
Other subjects
physics, health education
Inspired by
Smart Cane (Arduino Project Hub) a page on another site

Classroom carbon dioxide meter

Proposal

A meter shows the classroom's carbon dioxide level as traffic lights and records the measurements of the whole day.

Intermediateabout 8 lessonsAhjo 2

Problem
Poor ventilation makes people tired, but nobody in the classroom notices when the room needs airing.
You learn
reading a CO₂ sensor, recording measurements and drawing a graph, checking the sensor in outdoor air, designing the display
Materials
Arduino Uno, NDIR-type CO₂ sensor (to be acquired), RGB LED or a small display, 3D-printed case
Measure of success
In outdoor air the meter shows 400–450 ppm. It records the measurements of a whole lesson, and the graph shows the effect of airing.
Further development
The level is compared with the number of students and with opening the windows. A shared map is made from several classrooms.
Other subjects
chemistry, biology, health education

A sensory-friendly classroom

Proposal

A small display calmly tells when the noise or lighting in the classroom becomes too stressful.

Beginnerabout 6 lessonsAhjo 1Ahjo 2

Problem
Noise and bright light are a strain for some students, and the situation is noticed only after concentration is already lost.

The device records the day's sound levels, and the class agrees what the signal means in practice.

A rover for another planet

Proposal

A rover that avoids obstacles on its own, with its mass and parts budget limited as in a space probe.

Intermediateabout 8 lessonsAhjo 1Competitive robotics

Problem
A rover on Mars cannot be steered in real time, because a message takes minutes to arrive. The rover must avoid obstacles by itself.

Arduino Uno or a robotics kit, motor driver and two DC motors, distance sensor, body of cardboard or 3D-printed

The lightest load-bearing bridge

Proposal

A 3D-printed bridge designed to carry as large a load as possible with as small a mass as possible.

Beginnerabout 6 lessonsAhjo 1Ahjo 2

Problem
A structure must be made strong without wasting material. The same question is solved when designing bridges, aircraft and packaging.
You learn
truss structures, 3D modelling to dimensions, the effect of print orientation on strength, load testing
Materials
3D printer and PLA, masses, two tables or supports 20 cm apart
Measure of success
The bridge spans a 20 cm gap and weighs at most 30 g. The result is the ratio of the load held to the bridge's mass, and it improves from the first version.
Further development
Two truss types of the same mass are compared. The point where the structure breaks is predicted before the test.
Other subjects
physics, mathematics, visual arts
Inspired by
Straw Bridges Design Challenge (TeachEngineering) a page on another site

Automatic plant watering

Proposal

A system measures the moisture of a pot and waters the plant when the soil dries.

Intermediateabout 8 lessonsAhjo 2

Problem
The classroom plants dry out during holidays, and overwatering is as harmful as drought.
You learn
calibrating a soil moisture sensor, controlling a pump with a transistor, control with thresholds, keeping water safely away from electronics
Materials

Line-following robot with feedback control

Proposal

A robot follows a tape line, and its steering is improved with feedback control using measurements.

Demandingabout 10 lessonsAhjo 2Competitive robotics

Problem
A simple robot wobbles from side to side over the line and is slow. How can the steering be made smooth and fast?
You learn
reflectance sensors, P and PD control, tuning parameters by measuring, timing and comparison
Materials
Arduino Uno or a robotics kit, reflectance sensor array (3–5 sensors), motor driver and two motors, a track made of tape
Measure of success
The robot goes round the track without leaving it, and the lap time improves by at least 20 % from the first working version.
Further development
The robot recognises junctions and chooses its route. The control parameters are found with the help of a graph.

mathematics, physics

Crash test car with an accelerometer

Proposal

A model car crashes into an obstacle, and a micro:bit on board measures the passenger's acceleration.

Intermediateabout 6 lessonsAhjo 1Ahjo 2

Problem
How should the front of a car be built so that the force on the passenger in a crash is as small as possible?
You learn
measuring and recording acceleration, impulse and momentum, designing a crumple structure, repeated measurements
Materials
micro:bit, trolley or model car, ramp, crumple zones of cardboard or 3D-printed
Measure of success

Wind turbine blades

Proposal

The group designs and prints blades for a model turbine and finds out by measuring which shape gives the most power.

Intermediateabout 8 lessonsAhjo 2

Problem
The shape, angle and number of blades affect how much energy is taken from the wind. Which matters most?
You learn
3D modelling a blade, measuring power from voltage and current, an experiment with one variable, presenting results as a graph
Materials
3D printer, small DC generator or motor, load resistor, multimeter, fan
Measure of success
The group compares at least three blade versions with the same wind and load. The power of every version is measured three times.
Further development
The load resistor is varied to find the load that gives the most power. The result is compared with the power curve of a real wind turbine.

A prosthetic hand that feels its grip

Proposal

A 3D-printed hand whose fingers are moved by servos and whose grip stops when a force sensor feels the contact.

Demandingabout 12 lessonsAhjo 2

Problem
A prosthesis must be able to grip both a heavy and a fragile object without the user having to watch the hand all the time.
You learn

Materials
Arduino Uno, 2–5 servos, force sensor (FSR), fishing line, 3D-printed fingers and palm

The hand grips a plastic cup and lifts it without crushing it. The grip stops when the force sensor's reading goes over the chosen limit at least 9 times out of 10.
Further development
The hand is controlled with a muscle sensor or buttons. It is compared with real prostheses by price and functions.

biology, health education, philosophy

Model electric car

Proposal

The group builds an electric model car within a budget and improves its speed and energy use on the basis of measurements.

Intermediateabout 10 lessonsAhjo 2

Problem
In an electric car the motor, gearing, mass and air resistance together decide how far the battery takes you. What should be improved first?
You learn
motor and gear ratio, measuring speed and energy, 3D-printed wheels and gears, budgeting and reporting
Materials
DC motor, batteries, 3D-printed wheels and gears, body material, timing
Measure of success
The car travels 5 metres in a straight line. The group measures the average speed and the energy used, and the last version is at least 20 % better than the first by the chosen measure.
Further development
The group compares two gear ratios and calculates the car's efficiency. The cost calculation is presented as a sales pitch for a product.
Other subjects
physics, social studies
