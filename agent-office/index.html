    A READ-ONLY WINDOW

    A team, in miniature.

    This office represents Fysiikkalabra's developer and its independent science and pedagogy reviewers. Their roles come from the repository's existing agent definitions.

    Each role has a distinct human character, with different hair, clothing, colours and accessories. The publisher is a separate colleague; Lauri visits before making the publication decision.

    The replay uses the latest 200 reachable commits, including branch commits so separate review rounds are visible. Times are commit times in Europe/Helsinki. Each material panel describes that commit's own snapshot; a later timestamp on a different branch does not necessarily supersede an earlier version.

    A material goes to independent science and pedagogy review. Both must say PASS with the current content hash to clear the review gate. A content edit invalidates earlier approvals, and a FAIL returns the work for revision. Automated checks and Lauri's merge decision are separate requirements; this office does not infer successful CI or deployment from commits. A current legacy marker is a grandfathered prototype exception, not a cleared two-review gate.

    The optional Whole webpage review mode shows Lauri requesting a broader review from an external reviewer, selecting feedback and briefing Claude to make changes. It illustrates a user-described practice, not a recorded conversation or a third lesson approval.

    The office does not run agents, inspect private session transcripts, edit materials, trigger reviews, or publish anything. Office colleagues and carried material packets explain the workflow, not actual timed transfers between people.

    Material journeys follow recorded versions on the selected commit’s first-parent history. Each stage shows its own review evidence. Missing stages and specific reviewer comments are not invented.

    Speech and coffee breaks are playful workflow illustrations, not quotations or recorded agent activity. Commit messages and material titles stay in their original language.

    Inspired by Fysiklabbet's agent office. This implementation uses its own drawing and scripts, without the original site's analytics or dependencies.
