Electricity

0 / 16 lessons ready

Module goals

The student

  • can investigate electrical phenomena experimentally and make basic electrical measurements
  • can use the concepts of field and potential to describe the electric field
  • knows safety aspects related to electrical devices and the transmission of electrical energy
