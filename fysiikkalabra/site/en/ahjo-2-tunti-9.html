Lesson 9 · Test

Test plan and measurements

In your project now: What is measured, how precisely and how many repetitions. Stage 6: measurement results.

A lesson is a guiding model, not an order that binds every group. If your group is at another stage, agree the next step with the teacher and record in the journal what you actually did.

In this lesson you learn: You plan a test and collect results by measuring or observing.

Next task

Write a test plan and test the prototype with your measure of success three times. If the measure is a number, show the results as a table and a graph. If measuring a number does not suit the project, use a qualitative test, for example observation, a user's rating on a scale or before and after photos, and record the results just as carefully.

Test your own prototype only with machines and tools you have been inducted into and the teacher has allowed. If the prototype has electricity, moving parts or heat, agree the test with the teacher before you start.
