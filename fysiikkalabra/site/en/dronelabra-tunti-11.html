Lesson 11 · FPV, measuring and data

A graph of your own measurement data

Analysing flight video and measurement data with Python

Measurements from a table into Python: mean, spread and a graph that shows the conclusion.

In this lesson you learn: You process measurement data with Python and justify a conclusion with a graph.

Works with the present equipment

Start right away (5–10 min)

Run the ready-made Python program with the example data and see what graph it draws. Then replace the data with your own group's measurement file.

Analysing flight data with Python →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Read your group's measurements from a file, calculate the mean and the standard deviation and draw a graph that shows all the measurements and the mean.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Compare two series of measurements in the same graph and write one sentence on whether the difference is bigger than the variation in the measurements.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Measure the drone's position from a few frames of the video and calculate the speed at different moments. Compare the result with the average speed obtained with a stopwatch.

If you have to wait

If the computer is in use, check your table of measurements: does every number have a unit, and are the outlying values marked?

What did you make?

Basic level: A graph of your own measurement data, and the mean and standard deviation.

Challenge yourself: A comparison of two series of measurements, or a speed measured from video.

Write in your project diary

  • the graph and the statistics
  • the conclusion in one sentence
  • what you would measure differently

Together

Show your graph to your partner without explaining it. What do they conclude from it?

You need

  • your own computer with Python
  • the group's measurement files
  • a flight video filmed earlier

Step-by-step guide

  • Analysing flight data with Python
  • Mittauspisteiden kuvaaja in Finnish

Starter files

  • Analysis of series of measurements (Python) untested draft for the graph; the statistics were tried with the example data
  • Example data: two series of landings (CSV) practice template: invented data, not measured with a real drone

Safety

There is no flying in this lesson. Use only videos that show the flight area and the drone, not people. Do not share or publish them, and delete them from your computer when the work is done.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Ready-made program and example data 15 min, own data and graph 35 min, comparison or video 15 min, summary 10 min.

Preparation: The analysis is done on the students' own computers: ask them to install Python and the plotting library before the lesson, and try out the starter program yourself. Collect the groups' measurement files in one folder. Video analysis requires a video in which the camera stays still and a scale is visible in the picture.
