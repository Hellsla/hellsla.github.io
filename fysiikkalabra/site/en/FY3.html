FY3 · 2 cr · Optional

Energy and heat

16 / 16 lessons ready

Module goals

The student

  • deepens their understanding of energy as a central concept of physics
  • can investigate phenomena related to the thermodynamic state of matter and to changes of state
  • can apply the concepts and models of thermodynamics when examining energy production solutions and building a sustainable future
  • recognises the importance of energy balance and heat transfer in climate change

Lessons

  1. Force as a measure of interaction and weight G = mg
  2. Work and energy
  3. Thermodynamic system, state variables and temperature
  4. Determining absolute zero
  5. Pressure and hydrostatic pressure
  6. Internal energy, heat and conservation of energy
  7. Heating and cooling of a substance: Q = cmΔT
  8. Changes of state
  9. Thermal balance during a change of state
  10. Thermal expansion
  11. Changes of state of a gas and Boyle's law
  12. Equation of state of an ideal gas pV = nRT
  13. Work done by a gas and the heat engine
  14. Energy production and the efficiency of power plants
  15. Heat transfer, energy balance and climate change
  16. Applied lesson: heat phenomena in everyday life

Apply independently

At the end of the module you can try whether you can apply what you have learned independently, in the style of the matriculation examination. The model answers and marking guides open after your own solution.

  • Apply independently 3 tasks · 45 points
