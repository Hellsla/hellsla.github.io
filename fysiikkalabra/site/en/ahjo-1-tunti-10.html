Lesson 10 · Electronics and Arduino

A measuring device that draws

Sensors and inputs

Light-dependent resistor, voltage divider, analogue input and the serial plotter.

In this lesson you learn: You read a sensor with an analogue input and interpret the readings in the serial plotter.

Start right away (5–10 min)

Connect a light-dependent resistor and a 10 kΩ resistor as in the wiring picture, upload the ready sketch and open the serial plotter. Cover the sensor with your hand: the curve drops at once.

Light sensor and the serial plotter →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Send the sensor's readings to the serial port and save them.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Turn the sensor into an instrument or a light that reacts to its surroundings, for example a buzzer whose pitch rises when there is more light.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Calibrate the sensor, collect a series of measurements and plot a graph with units.

What did you make?

Basic level: Sensor readings in the serial plotter and your own device that reacts to the sensor.

Challenge yourself: A calibrated series of measurements and a graph with units.

You need

  • Arduino Uno and USB cable
  • light-dependent resistor and 10 kΩ resistor
  • breadboard and wires
  • buzzer or LED for the modify step

Step-by-step guide

  • Small extra challenge: A calibrated thermometer
