Lesson 15 · Your own project

Your own device

Mini project

A small device of your own with the methods you have learnt and ready-made solutions: building and testing.

In this lesson you learn: You build, test and improve your own device for one development round.

Start right away (5–10 min)

Gather the parts and get the first part working, for example a sensor or a light. If a part is waiting for the printer, build a cardboard or tape version first.

Project journal →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Build a working prototype by combining the course's examples and test it with your measure of success.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Change your solution based on the test, test again and note what the change did. A change that fails is a good result too, when you explain it.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Test the device with your measure three times, improve its weakest point and make it your own: case, colour, sound or shape.

If you have to wait

If the printer is busy, make a cardboard or tape version of the part and test the rest of the device with it. Record what the printed part has to withstand or fit.

What did you make?

Basic level: One round of development: a prototype, a test with the measure, a change based on the test and a new test, with a note of what you saw. The device does not have to succeed when the round is reasoned.

Challenge yourself: Three tests, the weakest point improved and a finished look.

Together

A short show and tell halfway through the lesson: show your unfinished device and ask one question you need help with.
