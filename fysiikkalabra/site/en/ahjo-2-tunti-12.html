Lesson 12 · Improve

Improvement 2

In your project now: An improvement whose effect shows in the measurements.

A lesson is a guiding model, not an order that binds every group. If your group is at another stage, agree the next step with the teacher and record in the journal what you actually did.

In this lesson you learn: You test whether a change improved the solution, and learn from an improvement that failed too.

Next task

Choose one improvement based on the test results and test its effect with the same measure or observation as before. The improvement can be a new part or sensor, but just as well a simpler structure, a different material or a better program. If the improvement does not work, that is a valuable result too: record what you tried, what you measured and what you learned.

Save in your project diary

A tested improvement and its reasons, also if the improvement did not work.

Maker snacks: Optional 10–15 minute experiments for the middle of the project work, lessons 8–12.

You need

  • measuring tools
  • materials for the improvement
