Grading tool

Ruukkilabra submissions are graded on your own computer with this separate page. It does not use the network, and no student data comes to Ruukkilabra. Choose EN at the top of the grading page to use it in English; the task statements stay in Finnish.

Download the grading page (arviointi-2026-27-p1.html)

Download the newest version whenever it is updated. The points and the imported submissions stay in the browser when you open the new version in the same browser.

How to grade

  1. Make a Forms form for each group, with a name that starts with the group (e.g. FY3.2 lv 2026-27 Palauta ratkaisut), and add its link to the page Submissions · Period 1 · 2026-27.
  2. Download the form's response folder (File responses) from Drive as a zip file.
  3. Open the grading page in Chrome or Edge and choose Tuo tiedosto (Import file) → the zip.
  4. Choose a task and grade its solutions one after another: the numbers 0 and 1 and the arrows ← → change the points, ↓ ↑ change the student.
  5. Export the points as a CSV file and back up the group to the school's Drive.
