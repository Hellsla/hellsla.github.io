# Courses, local courses, Jaksopohja, Ahjo (from CLAUDE.md 'Courses and scope')

Siirretty `fysiikkalabra/CLAUDE.md`:stä sellaisenaan (työjono 0b, 2026-11-17). Kaikki tämän tiedoston säännöt ovat voimassa; CLAUDE.md:ssä on niiden lyhyt muoto ja viite tähän tiedostoon. Sisältö on englanniksi kuten alkuperäinen.

- **Lukiokohtaiset opintojaksot** (school-specific courses such as FY10, teacher 2026-10-30) are not in `curriculum.json` and never get national goals: they live in `local-courses.json` (`scripts/local_courses.py`), render to `site/<CODE>.html` and the front page's own section; `docs/PORTAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` is the portal design, `docs/FY10_PLAN.md` the FY10 plan. Experimental investigations (`content/<lang>/tutkimukset/<id>/`, `scripts/investigations.py`) are one canonical page each, reviewed and published like a lesson, and listed by the courses that name them. The course page lists them as a bank with a search and filters (topic, F1–F3, E1–E3, working mode; `investigations.py bank`, `templates/invfilter.js`). **TVT-ohjeet** (`content/<lang>/tvt/<id>/`, `scripts/guides.py`, teacher 2026-10-31) are reusable skill guides (graphs, fitting, slope and its uncertainty, video analysis, spreadsheets, sensors, speed from data, the report), reviewed like an investigation and linked from it (`"guides"` in a tvt prerequisite, `[[ohje:<id>|label]]` in the text), never copied into it. Build later phases one reviewed PR at a time. **Course units** (`content/<lang>/units/<id>/`, `scripts/units.py`, teacher 2026-11-01) are the lessons of a local course written in its own language (FY14 *Road to Fusion*: English, with French; `docs/FY14_PLAN.md`, `docs/fy14/writing-guide.md`): the lesson template and inquiry stages, the course's components in `assets/units/<course>/`, published under the same three-review gate into that language's folder only, listed by chapter on every language's course page.
- **Jaksopohja** (teacher 2026-11-12; `jaksot/README.md`, `scripts/jaksot.py`, `test_jaksot.py`): every study unit outside the physics modules (Ahjo, Kilparobotiikka, LUMA-tutor, and any new one) is one folder `jaksot/<id>/`: `jakso.json` (its first key the common header `"jakso": {id, status, theme}`), `kohteet/` (one file per guide, workshop or project: a new item is a new file) and `<lang>/` for a translation, each file falling back to the Finnish. `jaksot.py` is the only place that knows the layout. A planned unit is a folder with `"front"` (its greyed row on the front page) and no code; a unit is written as `"pages"` of blocks and built from that alone by `jaksot.py`, whose block library (teksti, lista, kortit, linkit, and the parts Kilparobotiikka's pages were first built of: polku, kausi, paivakirja, taidot, roolit, turva-aiheet and the rest) any unit may use. Kilparobotiikka is written so since 2026-11-12 (its header names its course of the registry, `"course": "KILPAROBO"`; `kilparobo.py` keeps only the brief's rules, and its pages stayed byte for byte the same); `ahjo.py` and `lumatutor.py` remain the page modules of what the blocks do not cover yet. No unit's source lives at the root or under `content/` any more.
- **Ahjo Makerspace** (teacher 2026-11-08; `jaksot/ahjo/jakso.json` (was `ahjo.json`), `scripts/ahjo.py`, PORTAL_ARCHITECTURE "Ahjo Makerspace"): `ahjo.html`, `ahjo-materiaalit.html`, `ahjo-turvallisuus.html`, `ahjo-1.html`, `ahjo-2.html` in Kiviruukin lukio's colours with the original logo, never redrawn. Ahjo 1 and Ahjo 2 stay `_planned` in `local-courses.json`; a bank resource has one home and is linked, never copied; no laser cutter or CNC; safety topics stay "valmisteilla" until the school's responsible staff approve them. **Dronelabra** (teacher 2026-11-12; `docs/DRONELABRA_PLAN.md`) is Ahjo's third course in the same file (code `DRONELABRA`, pages `dronelabra.html` and `dronelabra-tunti-N.html`, its guides `ahjo-drone-*`, `ahjo-airbit-*`, `ahjo-fpv-lento`, its safety topics `drone-*`, its project cards in Ahjo's project bank): no parallel course system. A lesson may carry `status` (what it needs beyond the present equipment), `diary` and `teacher` {time, prep}; a project card `steps`, `safety` and `need`. Nothing is said about Air:bit 2, the camera drones or the flying rules that was not read in the maker's documents or on Traficom's and EASA's pages (the plan lists what was read and what is still unverified); the Air:bit is flown indoors only, the camera drone is never programmed, and indoor FPV is never presented as safe by default.
