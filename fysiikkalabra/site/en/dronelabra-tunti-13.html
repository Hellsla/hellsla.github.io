Lesson 13 · Your own drone project

Project plan

Coming up with and planning your own project

You choose a topic from the project bank or your own idea and write a plan whose success can be measured.

In this lesson you learn: You define a project whose success can be measured.

Works with the present equipment

Start right away (5–10 min)

Read the cards in the project bank and choose the two most interesting ones. For each, write one question that the project would answer.

Project journal →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Write the group's project plan: the research question or problem, the equipment needed, the work stages, a measurable success criterion and safety. Get the teacher to approve the plan.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Add a back-up plan to the plan: what do you do if a device is in use or the first solution does not work?

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Propose a project of your own that is not in the bank. Justify why it can be done with the school's equipment and how its success is measured.

What did you make?

Basic level: A project plan approved by the teacher, with a measurable success criterion.

Challenge yourself: A back-up plan, or a justified project idea of your own.

Write in your project diary

  • the project plan
  • the success criterion
  • the division of work and a schedule for three sessions

Together

Present the plan to another group in two minutes. They ask: how do you know that you have succeeded?

You need

  • project bank
  • project diary
  • a computer

Step-by-step guide

  • Project journal

Starter files

  • Test plan template (CSV) spreadsheet template

Safety

A project that involves flying is not started until the teacher has approved the safety section of the plan.

  • Drones: rules indoors and outdoors

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Project bank 15 min, plan 40 min, presentation to another group 10 min, approvals 10 min.

Preparation: Check which projects can be done with the current equipment and which require an additional purchase. Allow time for approving the plans and for making the equipment booking list.
