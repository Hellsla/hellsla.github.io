# Privacy

Ruukkilabra does not collect any information about students. The site has no server that receives your data, and no sign-in, analytics, ads or cookies. The maker of Ruukkilabra cannot see what you do on the site.

## What is saved in your browser?

So that your work is not lost when you close the page, Ruukkilabra saves some things only in this device's browser: your solutions and answers (solutions written in the editor with their formulas and pasted pictures, numerical answers, choices and written answers), your predictions and measurements, self-assessments, the times of tasks counted as done, lesson progress, concept card progress, and the read-aloud settings and voice model. When you save your first solution, the site asks the browser to keep the data persistently, so that the browser does not delete it by itself when disk space runs low.

Ruukkilabra does not send this data automatically to your teacher or anywhere else, and your teacher cannot see it in your browser.

## The work file

The *Save work to a file* button puts your work for the course into one file on your device. You can open the file on another device with *Open saved work*. The file contains everything the browser has saved from this course's pages:

- your solutions and answers, including the pictures you pasted into them as they are
- your self-assessments, including those of single tasks and the earlier levels of your goal assessments with their dates
- your predictions and measurements
- the tasks counted as done and their times, when your solutions were saved and when the file was made
- a random identifier and a checksum that are used to check that the file is intact.

The file does not contain your name or contact details, the read-aloud settings or work from other courses. Only paste pictures into a solution that you are happy to show your teacher.

## When you submit your work

The file reaches your teacher only if you send it yourself with your group's form. The forms are behind the *Submit* button. The form is in the school's Google Workspace (Google Forms and Google Drive) and is not part of Ruukkilabra. You sign in with your school account to send it, so the service attaches your name and school email address to the file. Your teacher can see everything in the file you submit.

Submitted work is handled in the school's teaching in the same way as work returned in the school's other services. The education provider is responsible for it, and your school's privacy notice tells you more. Ruukkilabra does not receive submitted files.

## How do I delete it?

You can clear the data saved in your browser at any time by deleting the site data in your browser settings (Cookies and site data). On a shared computer, clear it when you finish or use a private window. The work files you saved are ordinary files on your device, so delete them yourself. Clearing the browser does not delete a file you have already submitted.

## Outside services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs ordinary technical server data such as IP addresses. Videos in some courses come from YouTube and load only when you press play. Other outside links (for example photo sources and submission forms) open another website with its own rules.
