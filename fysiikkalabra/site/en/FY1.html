Physics as a natural science

0 / 8 lessons ready

Module goals

The student

  • becomes familiar with physics as a systematic science based on experiments
  • becomes familiar with the scales of matter and of the universe
  • becomes familiar with the methods used to gather information in physics
  • can plan and carry out simple scientific experiments
  • gains experiences that awaken and deepen an interest in physics and in studying it

Lessons

  1. Physics as a science and the scales of the universe coming soon
  2. Quantities, units and the SI system coming soon
  3. Measuring and the reliability of a measurement coming soon
  4. Graphs and fitting a straight line coming soon
  5. Uniform motion: s = vt coming soon
  6. Acceleration of free fall: v = gt coming soon
  7. Density: m = ρV coming soon
  8. Your own experimental investigation: planning, carrying out and reporting coming soon

Apply independently

At the end of the module you can try whether you can apply what you have learned independently, in the style of the matriculation examination. The model answers and marking guides open after your own solution.
