Lesson 6 · Build and program the Air:bit

Reading map of the program

Studying and modifying the drone's control program

How the drone program reads the sensors, calculates the attitude and shares the power between four motors.

In this lesson you learn: You read a ready-made program and say what its parts do.

Works with the present equipment

Start right away (5–10 min)

Open the drone's program in MakeCode and find the place where the radio message is received. Which five values does the controller send?

The Air:bit 2 radio controller and control program →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Follow the main loop of the program and make a reading map: reading the sensors, calculating the attitude, control and the motor speeds. Mark the place where the motors are stopped for safety reasons.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Change something that does not affect flight: for example what is shown on the display or when the battery warning appears. Test the change without propellers.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Explain from the formula for the motor speeds why, when tilting forwards, the power of two motors increases and that of two decreases.

If you have to wait

If the computer or the drone is in use, finish your controller program or write up a clean copy of the reading map.

What did you make?

Basic level: A reading map of the stages and safety functions of the drone program.

Challenge yourself: An explanation of how the program shares the power between four motors.

Write in your project diary

  • the reading map
  • the change you made and how you tested it
  • a part of the program you do not understand yet

Together

Explain one stage of your reading map to your partner. Your partner asks one question, and you look for the answer in the program together.

You need

  • a computer and MakeCode
  • the drone's micro:bit
  • USB cable
  • an assembled drone without propellers

Step-by-step guide

  • The Air:bit 2 radio controller and control program

Safety

All changes are tested without propellers. Values that affect flight are not changed yet.

  • Drones: propellers and motors

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Finding the radio handler 10 min, reading map 35 min, own change and test 20 min, summary 10 min.

Preparation: Make sure that the drone program opens in MakeCode on the school's computers. Save a copy of the original program so that it can be restored.
