# Privacy

Ruukkilabra does not collect any information about students for itself. The site has no sign-in, no analytics and no ads, and it uses no cookies.

## What is saved in your browser?

So that your work is not lost when you close the page, Ruukkilabra saves some things only in this device's browser: your answers to the tasks (solutions written in the editor, numerical answers, choices and written answers), your predictions and measurements, self-assessments, lesson progress, concept card progress, and the read-aloud settings and voice model. Ruukkilabra does not send this data automatically to your teacher or anywhere else, and your teacher cannot see it in your browser.

## When you submit your tasks

On a chapter page you can download a submission file yourself with the *Download all solutions* button. The file is saved on your device. It contains your answers to the chapter's tasks and a summary of your self-assessment of the chapter's goals: your latest assessment of each goal. Your self-assessments of single tasks and the history of your assessments are left out of the file and stay only in your browser.

The file reaches your teacher only if you send it yourself with a separate Google Forms form. The groups' forms are behind the *Submit* button. The form is not part of Ruukkilabra. Your teacher sees the answers and the summary of goal assessments in the file you submitted.

## How do I delete it?

You can clear the data saved in your browser at any time by deleting the site data in your browser settings (Cookies and site data). On a shared computer, clear it when you finish or use a private window. Clearing it does not delete a file you have already submitted.

## Outside services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs ordinary technical server data such as IP addresses. Videos in some courses come from YouTube and load only when you press play. Other outside links (for example photo sources and submission forms) open another website with its own rules.
