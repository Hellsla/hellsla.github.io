Mini project

In the mini project you combine techniques you have already practised and ready-made solutions into a small device of your own. You do not need to learn a new technique. Planning is done in lesson 14, building and testing in lesson 15, and lesson 16 starts with half an hour for finishing before the presentation. If you continue work you started during the course, you have more time for building. Note down ideas in the project journal during the course.

  • A lamp with your own light rhythm. An LED lantern or lamp whose cover and light rhythm you design yourself. Based on lessons 1 and 9.
  • A sensor instrument or light alarm. A device that plays, flickers or alarms according to a sensor reading. Based on lessons 9 and 10.
  • A moving figure or automaton. A cardboard figure or mechanism moved by a servo that reacts to a button or light. Based on lesson 11.
  • Your own measuring device. For example a light or temperature meter whose display or case you make yourself. Based on lessons 8 and 10.
  • A printed useful object. A holder, case or spare part made to measure and tested in real use. Based on lessons 3–6.

You can also carry out your own idea once the teacher has approved the plan.

You need

  • project diary
  • pen and paper for the sketch
  • your earlier course work
