## Quick start

FPV means flying with the help of the picture from the drone's camera. With the goggles on you see what the drone sees, but you do not see the room around you. That is why you never fly alone: the observer stands next to you and watches the drone the whole time.

1. Practise the motion controller's buttons with the device switched off.
2. Agree the commands Stop and Land with the observer.
3. Put the goggles on only when you are in your place and the safety officer has given permission.
4. Fly calmly in Normal mode and land on the mark.

### Try

Do one flight: take-off, hover, a slow flight to the mark and back, and landing. Act once as observer and as safety officer too.

### Modify

Fly the same route without the goggles and look at the drone directly. Compare which way was more precise.

### Challenge yourself

Record what information only the pilot sees from the goggles and what only the observer sees from the room.

## Challenge

Flying in the goggles feels easy, because the drone holds its position by itself. The difficulty is elsewhere: the pilot's picture of the space is narrow, and the safety of the whole group depends on the cooperation of the observer and the pilot. The goal is to fly so that the observer does not need to shout Stop even once.

Flying indoors is not automatically safe. The manufacturer urges particular caution indoors, because a lack of light or space can limit the drone's safety functions.

## Learning goals

After the work you can

- start, stop and land the drone with the motion controller
- act as observer and give clear instructions
- tell when the drone's sensors do not help
- compare FPV flight and flight in direct visual line of sight.

## Equipment and materials

The school's equipment is two camera drones, which come with FPV goggles and a motion controller. The drone belongs to the EU class C0, and its maximum take-off mass is $160\,\text{g}$.

| Supply | Note |
|---|---|
| Camera drone with its propeller guards | The manufacturer recommends using the propeller guards. |
| FPV goggles | Cleaned between users. |
| Motion controller | Controlled by moving your hand. The modes are Normal and Sport: only Normal mode is used. |
| Charged batteries | The battery is changed only when the drone is switched off. |
| Marker cones or tape marks | For marking the route and the landing place. |

With this controller the drone cannot be flown in a fully manual mode, and the drone cannot be programmed.

## Safety and induction

See the topics [FPV flight and the observer](ahjo-turvallisuus.html#drone-fpv), [flight area and roles](ahjo-turvallisuus.html#drone-lentoalue), [emergency stop](ahjo-turvallisuus.html#drone-hatapysaytys) and [filming](ahjo-turvallisuus.html#drone-yksityisyys). They are in preparation until the school has approved them.

- The observer stands next to the pilot, watches the drone with their own eyes for the whole flight and says at once if someone comes into the flight area or the drone approaches an obstacle. The pilot is still responsible for the flight.
- Flying is done only in Normal mode. In Sport mode and in trick flying, obstacle avoidance is switched off.
- The drone's sensors do not replace the pilot. The drone cannot avoid moving people. Glass, mirrors, white walls and repeating floor patterns can interfere with the sensors.
- If the drone loses its positioning, it starts to drift and does not stop precisely. Land at once then.
- The pilot stays in place in a clear space. The goggles are put on and taken off only when the drone is on the ground or hovering with the brake on.
- Stop at once if you feel dizzy or unwell. Take breaks between flights.
- Do not touch a flying drone and do not put your fingers inside the propeller guards.

## Steps

The motion controller's lock button is the most important button. The functions are from the manufacturer's user manual.

| What you want | How you do it |
|---|---|
| Start the motors | Press the lock button twice. |
| Take off | Hold the lock button down. The drone rises to a height of about $1.2\,\text{m}$ and stays hovering. |
| Brake and stay hovering | Press the lock button once. The same press again gives control back. |
| Land | Hold the lock button down while the drone is hovering. The drone lands and the motors stop. |
| Emergency stop | Press the lock button four times. The motors stop at once and the drone falls. Only in a real emergency. |

1. The safety officer goes through the checklist and makes sure that the flight area is clear.
2. The pilot takes their place, the observer next to them.
3. The pilot puts the goggles on and says Ready. The safety officer says Take-off.
4. Start the motors and take off.
5. Fly slowly to the mark and back.
6. Brake above the landing mark and land.
7. Take the goggles off only when the motors have stopped.
8. The observer tells one observation that the pilot did not see. Swap roles.

## Diagrams and example code

Draw a floor plan of the station: the flight area, the take-off and landing place, the route marks, the pilot's and the observer's place and the safety line. Mark on the plan the windows, mirrors and other surfaces that can interfere with the drone's sensors.

This guide has no program code, because the drone cannot be programmed.

## Test and measure

- How far from the mark did you land? Measure and record.
- How many times did the observer give an instruction during the flight?
- Fly the same route with the goggles and without. Compare the time and the precision of the landing.

The goggles can record the view on their own memory card, if one is installed. Times can be measured from the recording afterwards.

## If something does not work

- **The picture in the goggles cuts out or goes dark:** press the lock button once so that the drone brakes. The observer guides the landing verbally.
- **The drone drifts and does not hold its position:** land at once. The space may be too dim or the floor too uniform in colour.
- **The drone does not take off:** check the battery and that the propeller guards are fitted correctly. A wrongly fitted guard can cover the drone's front sensor.
- **The pilot feels dizzy:** Stop, brake, goggles off and a break. The observer or the teacher lands the drone.

## Extension

Design a precision course for next time: three marks that are flown around, and a landing mark. Size the course to fit inside the flight area and leave at least the gap decided by the teacher to the safety line.

## Document your work

Attach to your project diary

- the roles in which you acted
- the distance of the landing from the mark
- the observer's observation of your flight
- a comparison: goggles and direct visual line of sight.

## For the teacher: preparation

The information was read from the manufacturer's user and safety instructions on 12 November 2026. Read the instructions yourself before the first flight: in class C0, familiarising yourself with the user manual is the duty of the person flying.

- The manufacturer states that the product is not intended for children, and gives no age limit. The intended use stated by the manufacturer is private use. The school decides on use in teaching.
- The goggles do not meet the requirement of direct visual line of sight. Under the EU rules, a person flying with goggles must have next to them an observer who watches the drone without any aids and can speak to the pilot directly.
- The manufacturer urges users of the goggles to take breaks and to stop if they feel unwell, and advises people with a serious underlying illness to ask a doctor before use. Agree how a student can tell about an obstacle confidentially. All the tasks can be done without the goggles.
- Using the drone may require logging in to the manufacturer's app and activating the device. Do these before the lesson with the school's account.
- Check the setting of the motion controller's emergency stop in the app and practise braking and landing yourself.
- The drone's own flight data is stored in the device, but the manufacturer does not describe a way of exporting it as a table. That is why the measurements are made with a tape measure, a stopwatch and from video.
- With two drones, flight turns come round slowly. Organise stations: FPV flight, Air:bit measurement, data processing and course design.
- Flying outdoors requires registration, marking the registration number and checking the airspace. See the [safety induction](ahjo-drone-perehdytys.html).
