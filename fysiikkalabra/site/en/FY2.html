Module goals

Show all 4 goals

The student

  • becomes familiar with energy as a central concept of physics
  • knows different forms of energy and ways of producing energy
  • can compare the orders of magnitude of different ways of producing energy and their environmental effects
  • gains the skills to take part in discussion and decision-making about the environment and technology from the point of view of a sustainable energy economy
