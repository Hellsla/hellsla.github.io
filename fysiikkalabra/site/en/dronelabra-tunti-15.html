Lesson 15 · Your own drone project

Measurement results and an improved version

Testing and improving the project

The project is tested by measuring, improved and tested again.

In this lesson you learn: You test your solution by measuring and improve it on the basis of the results.

Needs the teacher's safety approval

Start right away (5–10 min)

Read your success criterion aloud and straight away make one measurement that tells you how far you are from it.

Flight tests and measuring →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Carry out the planned series of measurements, record the results and compare them with the success criterion. Make one change and measure again.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Draw a graph of the results before and after the change and write whether the change improved the result.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Assess the biggest source of error in your measurements and suggest how it could be reduced.

If you have to wait

If the device is in use, process the data you have already collected and start putting the presentation together.

What did you make?

Basic level: Measurement results before and after one change. An unsuccessful change is also accepted when it has been measured and justified.

Challenge yourself: A graph of the results, or an assessment of the sources of error.

Write in your project diary

  • the measurement results and the graph
  • the change you made and its effect
  • what you would do if there were more time

Together

Another group repeats one of your measurements. What causes the difference between their result and yours?

You need

  • the project equipment
  • measuring instruments
  • a computer
  • charged batteries, if you fly

Step-by-step guide

  • Flight tests and measuring
  • Analysing flight data with Python
  • Project journal

Starter files

  • Flight log (CSV) template for a spreadsheet
  • Analysis of series of measurements (Python) untested draft for the graph; the statistics were tried with the example data

Safety

A changed drone or program is tested first without propellers or at low height, as in earlier lessons.

  • Drones: propellers and motors
  • Drones: charging and storing batteries
  • Drones: flight area and roles
  • Drones: emergency stop and abnormal situations

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: First measurement 10 min, series of measurements 25 min, change and new measurement 25 min, diary 15 min.

Preparation: Have measuring instruments ready and book flying turns. Remind students that what counts in the assessment is measured and justified development, not just a working end result.
