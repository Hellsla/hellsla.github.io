How you study in Ruukkilabra

Choose a module and start from its first chapter, or from the chapter your class is working on. Every chapter follows the same stages.

  1. Think about ita phenomenon and the question the chapter answers
  2. Predictcommit to your own prediction before the results
  3. Investigateexperiment with a simulation or data and record what you see
  4. Theory summarythe explanation and the formula are built from the observations
  5. Applyuse the model in a new situation
  6. Check your understandingtest what you can do; the exercises are at the end of the chapter
More study tips
  • Predict first: once you have committed to an answer, you notice what is surprising in the investigation.
  • Use the simulation like an experiment: change one thing at a time and record what happens.
  • Open an answer or a model solution only after you have tried yourself, and compare yours with it step by step.
  • Do the core exercises; the others are extra practice, and level 3 is challenging application.
  • At the end of a chapter, go on to Next chapter.
