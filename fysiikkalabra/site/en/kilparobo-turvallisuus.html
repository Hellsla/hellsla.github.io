Competitive robotics

Safety in competitive robotics

Everyone completes the safety induction before using tools or driving the robot. This page is a checklist. The binding instructions come from the teacher.

Draft The safety instructions are a draft until the school's responsible staff have approved them.

Anyone may say STOP. When someone says STOP, the robot is stopped at once.

Who decides what

Student mentor

  • shows the working methods in the instructions
  • reminds others of the safety instructions
  • stops a dangerous situation and tells the teacher

The teacher decides

  • approves the use of tools and gives permission to work independently
  • decides the rules of the practice and test area
  • decides how batteries are charged and stored
  • decides what to do in an emergency

A student mentor cannot give permission to use dangerous tools independently.

The robot's mechanical movements

  • Gears, chains, arms and grippers can pinch fingers. Keep hands, hair, jewellery and cables away from moving parts.
  • When the robot is powered on, assume that it can move.
  • Repair and adjust the robot only with the power off.

The teacher decides: where the robot may be used and who may drive it

Handling batteries

  • Use only the V5 Robot Battery and charge it with the V5 charger.
  • Do not use a battery that is dented, swollen, hot or smells. Take it to the teacher.
  • Do not leave a charging battery unattended.
  • Store batteries where the teacher says, so that their connectors cannot touch metal.

The teacher decides: where batteries are charged and stored and how a damaged battery is handled

Tools used for building

  • Hand tools such as hex keys and spanners are used after the induction.
  • Cutting and filing metal parts leaves sharp edges. File the edges smooth and wear safety glasses.
  • Power tools such as a cordless drill or a rotary multi-tool are used only with the teacher's permission and after the teacher's induction.

The teacher decides: the use of each power tool, and its independent use separately

Testing the robot

  • Test a new program first with the wheels off the ground, or in a fenced-off area.
  • Agree before the test who holds the controller and who stops the robot.
  • Say it aloud when the robot starts.

The teacher decides: the test area and whether autonomous programs may be tested without the teacher

Safety of the practice area

  • Keep the field and walkways clear of bags, parts and cables.
  • Spectators stay outside the edge of the field.
  • Put parts and tools back at the end of every session.

The teacher decides: the limits and rules of the practice area

Switching the robot off and making it safe

  • Stop the program from the controller or the Brain's screen.
  • Switch the Brain off with its power button.
  • Disconnect the battery when the robot is repaired, carried or stored.
  • Lower raised arms and mechanisms or support them so that nothing falls.

The teacher decides: what to do in an emergency and whom to tell
