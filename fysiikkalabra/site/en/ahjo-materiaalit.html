Small challenges 12

Challenges of 15–45 minutes to do alongside Ahjo 1's lessons. Each has three levels: Try, Modify and Challenge yourself. They include visual arts, music, design and crafts too.

A light-up paper circuit card

A card whose LED lights when you fold a corner shut.

30 minAhjo 1, lesson 1: Welcome to Ahjovisual arts

You need: card, copper tape, 3 V coin cell, LED, tape

  • Try Stick two tracks of copper tape on the card, put the LED and the coin cell between them and fold a corner into a switch.
  • Modify Draw or cut a picture on the cover that the light shines through.
  • Challenge yourself Add a second LED in parallel. Explain why both shine equally brightly.

Your own stamp

A stamp modelled in Tinkercad with your own pattern or initials.

30 minAhjo 1, lesson 3: Basics of 3D modellingvisual artsdesign

You need: computer with Tinkercad, stamp ink (after printing)

  • Try Model a $30\,\text{mm}$ disc with a raised pattern or letters on it.
  • Modify Make your own pattern or logo and add a handle to the stamp.
  • Challenge yourself Remember to mirror the letters. Explain why the stamp's print is a mirror image.

A paper bridge

A bridge folded from one A4 sheet that spans a $20\,\text{cm}$ gap.

30 minAhjo 1, lesson 4: A model to measuredesignphysics

You need: A4 paper, two stacks of books, coins or weights, scales

  • Try Fold a bridge from the sheet and add coins on it until it gives way.
  • Modify Try another shape, such as a concertina, a tube or an arch, and compare the results.
  • Challenge yourself Weigh the largest load the bridge held and work out how many times its own mass it carried.

Guess and weigh

Your own estimate of a print's mass, checked on the scales.

15 minAhjo 1, lesson 6: Judging and fixing a printmathematicsphysics

You need: finished prints, PrusaSlicer, scales reading to $0.1\,\text{g}$

  • Try Guess the mass of three prints before weighing them, and write the guesses down.
  • Modify Compare your guess with the slicer's estimate and the reading on the scales.
  • Challenge yourself Work out the material cost of a print from the mass and price of the spool.

A sewn light-up badge

A felt badge whose LED lights through a circuit sewn with conductive thread.

45 minAhjo 1, lesson 7: Circuits and componentscraftsvisual arts

You need: felt, conductive thread, coin cell and holder, LED, needle

  • Try Sew the battery holder and the LED onto the felt so that the plus and minus tracks do not touch.
  • Modify Design the badge's shape and pattern yourself and make the LED part of the pattern.
  • Challenge yourself Measure the thread's resistance with a multimeter and estimate how long a thread still works.

A measurement poster

An infographic of the class's battery measurements.

30 minAhjo 1, lesson 8: Measuring with a multimetervisual artsmathematics

You need: the class's measurements, paper and pens or a spreadsheet

  • Try Collect the class's battery measurements in one table with units.
  • Modify Make a poster of the results in which the picture tells the result without long text.
  • Challenge yourself Work out the mean and the range and mark them on the poster.

A buzzer tune

The Arduino plays a familiar tune or your own on a piezo buzzer.

30 minAhjo 1, lesson 9: A first Arduino programmusicphysics

You need: Arduino Uno, piezo buzzer, wires

  • Try Connect the buzzer to pin 8 and play one note with tone(8, 440, 500).
  • Modify Write an eight-note melody and change the tempo.
  • Challenge yourself Work out the frequencies of a scale with $f = 440\,\text{Hz} \cdot 2^{n/12}$ and play a major scale.

A Morse sender

A button that sends a message as light and sound.

30 minAhjo 1, lesson 10: Sensors and inputslanguageshistory

You need: Arduino Uno, push button, LED and resistor, piezo buzzer

  • Try Build a button that lights the LED and sounds the buzzer when pressed.
  • Modify Send your partner a short message in Morse code and let them decode it.
  • Challenge yourself Program the Arduino to tell a short press from a long one and print them in the serial monitor.

A calibrated thermometer

A thermometer whose reading you can trust.

45 minAhjo 1, lesson 10: Sensors and inputsphysicschemistry

You need: Arduino Uno, temperature sensor from the starter kit, iced water and warm water, reference thermometer

  • Try Read the sensor's raw values in the serial monitor.
  • Modify Show the temperature with LEDs or sound so that you can read it without looking at a screen.
  • Challenge yourself Calibrate the sensor at two points with iced and warm water, and estimate the uncertainty of the measurement.

A bristle robot

A small robot made from a toothbrush head and a vibration motor.

30 minAhjo 1, lesson 11: Motors and actuatorsdesignphysics

You need: toothbrush head, vibration motor, coin cell, double-sided tape

  • Try Fix the motor and the battery onto the brush head and set the robot going.
  • Modify Give the robot its own look and build it a track or an arena.
  • Challenge yourself Measure the robot's speed with three repetitions and find out how the angle of the bristles affects it.

A cardboard automaton

A cardboard figure that moves when you turn a crank.

45 minAhjo 1, lesson 12: Building a robotvisual artsdesignphysics

You need: cardboard, skewers, hot glue, scissors

  • Try Build a box with an axle through it. Fix an off-centre disc on the axle that lifts the figure.
  • Modify Design the figure and its movement, for example a jump, a nod or a wave.
  • Challenge yourself Change the disc to an oval or a polygon and draw how the figure's height changes during one turn.

A logo sticker for your project

Your own logo cut as a sticker on the vinyl cutter.

45 minAhjo 1, lesson 14: Planning the mini projectvisual artsentrepreneurshipNeeds the device induction

You need: vinyl cutter (after the device induction), sticker vinyl, transfer tape

  • Try Draw a simple one-colour logo for your mini project.
  • Modify Adjust the logo so that it still works small and cut.
  • Challenge yourself Compare two sizes and find the smallest detail the cutter still cuts cleanly.
