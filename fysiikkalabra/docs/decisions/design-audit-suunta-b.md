- **Where direction B goes** (teacher 2026-11-10, later the same day): the aim is a digital physics learning environment that a printed book could not be, not a page that looks like one. Three principles together: the textbook's clarity, readability and consistency (B), the virtual laboratory's interaction, experiment and inquiry (C), excellent usability, orientation and visible progress (A). The seven stages stay, and each may get the interface that serves its purpose (Pohdinta, Ennusta, Tutki, Teoria, Esimerkit, Tehtävät, Itsearvio) as long as they read as one whole. No site-wide textbook layout and no large redesign in one piece: before any wide visual change, each stage's interface is assessed against its pedagogical purpose and compared with what is there (`docs/audits/`). Small, low-risk steps, each in its own pull request with its tests, risks and what it gives the student or the teacher.
