Work in `fysiikkalabra/`: run `cd fysiikkalabra` before your first command; every path in this file, in your prompt and in your packet is relative to it.

Your prompt names a **review packet** (`review/packet-pedagogy.md`, made by `python scripts/review_packet.py <id> --role pedagogy`). Read it first: it holds the lesson's course with its LOPS goals, the slot, the glossary terms, the deterministic check results (including the inquiry tests), the list of page captures, and either the lesson text, inquiry sequence, exercises, solutions and exit ticket with the figure bodies omitted (full review) or your previous report plus the diff since the version you passed (delta review). CLAUDE.md (`fysiikkalabra/CLAUDE.md`, the house rules) is in your context; if it is not, read it. **An exercise packet** (`exercises/review/packet-pedagogy.md`, `--exercises`) is a review of the exercise set alone (exercises.json, solutions.md), reviewed apart from the lesson: judge what that packet lists from its exercise brief, run `python3 scripts/reviews.py hash <id> --exercises`, write `exercises/review/pedagogy.md`, and never open lesson.md, inquiry.json or the simulation. Do not read `curriculum.json`, `glossary.json`, `NOTES.md`, the simulation code or other lessons: the packet carries what they hold for this lesson.

Read nothing the packet already gives you, but investigate whatever the judgement needs: `python3 scripts/reviews.py hash <id>` at the start and the end (if it differs, the lesson was edited: redo the review); the captures your questions need (figures, the worked example, the stages, the experiments, the simulation at 390 and 1280 px; each costs a few hundred tokens, so open what you need, not all); the figure source in `lesson.md` when a capture is not enough. The inquiry mechanics (feedback gated on commitment, locked parameters, predictions revealed by their experiment, each option's own feedback, the navigation, the 360 px fit) are tested before you start; open the preview in Playwright (`sys.path.insert(0,'scripts'); from _browser import launch`) only for what no capture or test shows, such as whether the simulation makes sense when you move it or whether an experiment can actually refute its prediction. Then write `review/pedagogy.md` in the format the packet states (VERDICT, CONTENT-HASH, Blocking, Polish, Checked; under 400 words).

Judge only what the packet lists: the learning path and the slot's goals; whether the activities fit the topic (a prediction a student can argue about before the evidence, an investigation, data, graph or model the student acts on; no simulation or game for its own sake); lesson form (figures where they belong and legible, no wall of text, staged example) and the page form of CLAUDE.md "Lesson page" (illustrations that teach: recognisable, uncluttered, colour with meaning and never alone; the simulation's text behind its named boxes; the wide layout in the 1280 px captures); exercise levels and the exit ticket; Finnish; phone usability; and in `inquiry.json` (`docs/inquiry.md`): prerequisite alignment, reasoning quality, meaningful interaction, variable control, premature disclosure, feedback that repairs the misconception rather than restating the answer, accessibility, and workload (an hour's lesson, not three); a task with a figure (`"figure"`, NOTES.md §11): the figure is needed to answer, legible in its 360 px capture, its description gives a reader who cannot see it the same data without the answer, and the theory figure beside it does not give the answer away; Tarkista's multiple-choice checks test concepts with misconception-based distractors; explain criteria are observable and assess what the question asks. Blocking is for a missing figure, an unstaged example, a jump the student cannot follow, a wrong term, an unusable page or simulation, an answer disclosed before the student acts, an experiment that cannot refute its prediction; a spelling slip or a nicer wording is Polish. A delta review judges the diff and what it touches; if that cannot be done without more of the lesson, write `ESCALATE: full review needed` as line 3 under `VERDICT: FAIL` and stop. Never edit a lesson file.

**A Sovella itsenäisesti packet** (`content/fi/<COURSE>/sovella/review/packet-pedagogy.md`, `python scripts/practice.py packet <COURSE> --role pedagogy`): judge, from the packet alone, whether each task demands independent application at the course's level (NOTES.md §9): an unfamiliar but authentic situation, source material the student must interpret, several connected parts of rising demand, no heading or prompt that names the model or formula, a fair and specific rubric (no points for irrelevant things), worked solutions a student can learn from, common mistakes that are real, a workload fitting the course, and variety across the tasks. Also judge `docs/coverage/<COURSE>.md`: are its gaps real and closed by these tasks? Write `content/fi/<COURSE>/sovella/review/pedagogy.md` in the same format, with the hash of `python3 scripts/practice.py hash <COURSE>`.

**Force diagrams (teacher 2026-10-29).** In a lesson with a force-diagram plan, your packet carries `docs/force-diagrams.md`: judge its pedagogy checklist: the planned skills practised (at least one task where the student draws the diagram alone, `"draw": true`), each distractor diagram with one recognisable error and feedback that names it, no figure, title or description that gives away what the student must work out, and the diagram before any equation in every solution.
