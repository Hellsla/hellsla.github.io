# Fysiikkalabra — house rules for every agent

Short form (work queue 0b, 2026-11-17): the rules in force. Dated decisions and implementation detail are in
`docs/decisions/`, named in each section; the full former text of each moved paragraph is there word for word, and
`docs/decisions/README.md` maps every former rule to its place. No rule was changed or removed.

## What this repo is
A digital textbook for Finnish upper-secondary physics (LOPS 2019): theory, simulations and exercises, one folder per lesson under `content/fi/<course>/<lesson-id>/` (its texts; the language-neutral code is in `lessons/<course>/<lesson-id>/`, interface texts in `lang/fi.json`, README "Languages"). `scripts/build.py` renders the lessons whose reviews are current into `site/`, which CI builds and publishes at `https://ruukkilab.fi/` (the `site` artifact of the CI run on main, pushed to the root of the public repository Hellsla/ruukkilabra by `.github/workflows/ruukkilabra-deploy.yml`; work queue 0g PR 2, 2026-11-22; teacher 2026-11-04). `site/` is git-ignored and not committed (work queue 0g PR 3, 2026-11-23). Published pages must never name or link the teacher's other sites (hellsla.github.io); `scripts/deploy_site.py` fails CI if they do. Links inside site/ stay relative. Previews (`build.py <id>` or `--all`) go to the git-ignored `preview/`.

## Courses and scope
- `curriculum.json` is the single source for every course (FY1–FY8): its LOPS 2019 goals and central contents from `docs/lops2019/`, its lesson slots, and optional `scope_notes`, `exercise_levels`, `initialized`. `scripts/courses.py` turns a lesson id into its course, slot and folders; nothing else hardcodes a course.
- Stay inside the course's central contents and scope notes (your packet carries them). Grep the MAOL guide in `docs/lops2019/` when unsure whether something belongs to a course.
- **Lessons per course** (teacher 2026-10-26): FY1 and FY2 have 8 lessons each, FY3–FY8 16 each (`planned_lessons`). `/new-course` notes a course whose slots do not match; the missing slots come from the teacher, never from an agent. `/new-course <FY4>` prepares a course (`scripts/new_course.py`); it never writes goals or slots.
- **School-specific courses** (FY10, FY14, Ahjo, Dronelabra, Kilparobotiikka, …) are not in `curriculum.json` and never get national goals: `local-courses.json` (`scripts/local_courses.py`), their own pages and front-page section. Investigations, TVT guides and course units are one canonical page each, reviewed and published like a lesson under the same three-review gate. Every study unit outside the physics modules is one folder `jaksot/<id>/` (`jaksot/README.md`; `scripts/jaksot.py` is the only place that knows the layout); no unit's source lives at the root or under `content/`. Ahjo: a bank resource has one home and is linked, never copied; no laser cutter or CNC; safety topics stay "valmisteilla" until the school's responsible staff approve them; nothing is said about Air:bit 2, the camera drones or the flying rules that was not read in the maker's documents or on Traficom's and EASA's pages; the Air:bit is flown indoors only, the camera drone is never programmed, indoor FPV is never presented as safe by default. Build later phases one reviewed PR at a time. Detail: `docs/decisions/kurssit-ja-jaksot.md`, `docs/PORTAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Audience and language
- Students aged 16–19, smart but meeting the idea for the first time. Site language Finnish; agent prompts and reports may be in English.
- Physics terms come from `glossary.json`; add a missing term there, do not invent synonyms.
- **Selkeä suomi** (`content/fi-easy/`, for S2 students; teacher 2026-11-03): the same physics in clearer Finnish, difficult words explained the first time, concrete examples, clear instructions, and 3–5 *Luvun tärkeät käsitteet* in `meta.json` `key_concepts`; NOTES.md §12 is the rule set, `check_i18n.py` checks it.

## Physics and maths conventions
- SI units, upright with a thin space: `9,81 m/s²`. Decimal comma in Finnish text, decimal point in JSON.
- **Every number with its unit is LaTeX, in prose too** (teacher 2026-11-01; NOTES.md §1): `$10\,\text{N}$`, `$1{,}18\,\text{kN}$`, `$9{,}81\,\text{m/s}^2$`, `$4{,}0\,\%$`, in every language and every text a student reads; only an `aria-label` stays plain. `lint_lesson.py` fails an edited lesson on a plain one; reviewers report one in new or edited text as Major.
- g = 9,81 m/s², always in m/s² (never N/kg). Three significant figures in final answers unless the data has fewer.
- Vectors with an overline in LaTeX (`\overline{F}`, `\overline{F}_{\mu}`; teacher 2026-10-29, never `\mathbf` or `\vec`; `scripts/test_notation.py` checks every text, figure, simulation and interface string in CI), magnitudes plain. Every formula appears once in display LaTeX with its symbols named.
- **Significant figures** (teacher 2026-11-15, P7; NOTES.md §2): 230 V has three significant figures; a rated or measured value's trailing zeros are significant unless the task says otherwise (480 kW: three), but the context decides: a time span the task sets, such as 10 min, is exact and does not limit the answer (teacher 2026-11-16); a limit value is rounded to the safe side (an "at most" value down, an "at least" value up: 10,5 kW at most → 10 kW, 1,511 kg at least → 1,6 kg with two figures).
- **Values and terms** (teacher 2026-11-15, P8 and P9; NOTES.md §2): a constant or material value is MAFY's whenever the MAFY tables have one, otherwise the value with its source behind a link. Small differences that are not contradictions stay as they are (P9: ice 334 kJ/kg, water 4190 J/(kg·K), sound 340 or 343 m/s, Mars 3,71 m/s², Am-241 432 a, C-14 5700 a); only clear contradictions and new content follow the MAFY rule. Friction is **liukukitka** (liukukitkakerroin; en sliding friction), never liikekitka; on a body at rest **lepokitka**, and its greatest value **täysin kehittynyt lepokitka eli lähtökitka** (en maximum (fully developed) static friction), the coefficient lepokitkakerroin; "lepokitka on enintään μ₀N" stays.
- **Energy into heat** (teacher 2026-11-15, P1; `docs/physics-guardrails.md`): from FY3 on, energy never "muuttuu lämmöksi" or "jää lämmöksi": it becomes internal energy, which is transferred to the surroundings as heat ("muuttuu sisäenergiaksi, joka siirtyy lämpönä ympäristöön"). Lämpöteho and "siirtyy lämpönä" are right; a wrong option and a described misconception keep their words; FY2 keeps MAOL's "lämpöenergia". `physics_phrases.py` warns about "lämmöksi" and "liikekitka" (`docs/physics-phrases-known/<course>.tsv` lists the sentences work queue 18 still corrects; delete a row with its correction).
- Every exercise has `answer`, `unit` and `tolerance`; the data carries the significant figures the answer should have, and a student rounding to them stays inside the tolerance. `answers.py` computes every exercise answer; `lesson-answers.py` the numbers of the lesson text and the inquiry.

## Lesson form (human decisions 2026-10-25, every lesson)
1. **Visualise.** `## Ydinajatus` and `## Kaava: <law>` each have at least one inline SVG figure right after the paragraph it explains, and any other theory point with a picture (p = F/A) gets a simple one; every label inside a figure, and every label and value in a simulation's canvas and readout, is LaTeX (`foreignObject`, NOTES.md §1, §3); graphs and figures are of printed-textbook quality (NOTES.md §3); no paragraph over about 120 words.
2. **Solve symbolically first, then substitute** values with units, in the worked example and every solution; solutions are written in LaTeX (NOTES.md §1).
3. **Simulations are visually rich, not slider-rich.** Keep the physics controls; spend the effort on the picture, motion, meaningful colour, labels, a legend. Every animated sim has a speed control (hidas · normaali · nopea) and only labels and values on the canvas; explanations go behind closed boxes under it.
4. **Worked examples are staged**: seven stages (rule 7) separated by `<!-- vaihe -->` lines, each with a bold lead-in and one step; the page reveals them one at a time.
5. **Inquiry first, shaped by the topic** (`docs/inquiry-spec.md`; no fixed template): every lesson has `inquiry.json` (`docs/inquiry.md`) through Pohdinta → Ennusta → Tutki → Teoria → Esimerkit → Tehtävät → Testaa ja arvioi (keys `herata`, `ennusta`, `tutki`, `selita`, `sovella`, `tarkista` stay): a phenomenon, a retrieval of prior learning, a committed prediction before the evidence where the topic allows, the evidence that fits the topic (a guided experiment on a simulation only when manipulating it reveals something; otherwise a table, a graph, competing explanations or a model to build), the model, a transfer task with hints and common-mistake feedback, three checks. A simulation or a game is there for what it teaches, never for show. Feedback is gated on commitment, never access; no answer is disclosed before the student has acted; theory stays readable on its own.
6. **Chapter names, not ids.** A student never sees a lesson id: "luvussa *Paine ja hydrostaattinen paine*", not "oppitunnilla fy3-4".
7. **A complete model solution for every worked example and every task** (teacher 2026-10-27): a final answer or a formula alone is not enough. Each solution shows (1) what is given, with units, and what is asked; (2) the principle and why it fits the situation; (3) the starting equation and the asked quantity solved in symbols before any number; (4) the unit conversions needed (or that none are); (5) the substitution with units and enough intermediate steps; (6) the answer, rounded to the data's precision, with its unit and why that many figures; (7) a check that the result is physically reasonable. A task answered in words gets a justified model answer; a graph task shows how the graph is read. An exercise's solution and every `numeric` task's solution sit behind that task's own *Näytä ratkaisu* button. `meta.solutions` is `all`. Every language version (an S2 version included) is as complete. `scripts/check_solutions.py` (lint and CI) reports every missing or incomplete solution; NOTES.md §3 "Complete solutions".
NOTES.md says how to write each; `scripts/lint_lesson.py` and `scripts/test_inquiry.py` check what they can.

Fix a layout problem in the shared components (`templates/site.css` header is the design system), never per lesson.

Exceptions: the student's own "Avaa tehtävät teorian viereen" panel from 1100 px (not the default), the illustration beside "Tällä tunnilla opit", a figure beside the paragraph it explains when the two are about as tall (else under it), the simulation beside the experiments. The worked example's steps run under its problem. One type scale (title 32–36 px, stage 24, task 19, text 18 on a 1.6 line, inside a task 17, interface 15, meta 14 px); the stage bar is the progress bar (sticky; under 700 px one button "3/7 Tutki").

## Readability (teacher 2026-10-27, every lesson; NOTES.md §8)
- **Text**: one main idea per paragraph (about 120 words at most), short content-naming `###` subheadings where a section holds several ideas, bold only for a key definition or conclusion, no extra coloured boxes. Never remove a physical reason or causal step to shorten text: restructure instead.
- **Opening**: `learn` in inquiry.json holds 2–3 one-line goals from the student's side that complete *Tällä tunnilla opit*: a lowercase -maan/-mään verb, parallel when joining skills ("laskemaan …"; English "In this lesson, you will learn to" + "calculate …"); never "Osaat …" there. Module goals ("Opiskelija osaa …"), *Pohjatiedot* and rubrics keep their own grammar. `scripts/objectives.py` checks it; `prior` is a list (*Pohjatiedot*), each item ending "(luku *<chapter>*)"; one Miksi question (or two short sentences).
- **Figures** right after the paragraph, table or list they explain, never apart from it; no figure after a long block it does not explain.
- **Calculations** as one inline chain per step in the source; the page shows long ones as aligned rows that wrap on a phone, the answer set apart and the reasonableness check as its own block (`scripts/mathlayout.py`). Never split a chain by hand for the layout.
- **Tasks**: the situation first, the question last as its own sentence; feedback, hints and solutions open inside the task's card.
- **Checks**: `check_layout.py` (in `check_lesson.py` and CI) fails a page with overflow, overlapping blocks, cards out of reading order, a large empty column beside an opened card or a figure, over-long lines, text under 17 px or controls under 40 px, or a heading under the sticky navigation, in every open state; fix the shared component and add a check when a new kind of problem appears (NOTES.md §6). `screenshot.py <id>` gives the captures of the opened states.
- **Open solutions at 360 px before any review** (teacher 2026-11-15): the developer runs `python scripts/check_lesson.py <id>` in the session (layout at 360, 390, 768 and 1280 px; never through `ci_env.py run`, whose image has no Node for the KaTeX check) or `python scripts/ci_env.py run python scripts/check_layout.py <id> --width 360` (it opens every solution in every language) and fixes every clipped formula in the shared component or the text before the reviewers are asked; a calculation row or inline formula that has to be scrolled to is clipped. Only a re-break of a calculation chain (same numbers, signs and units in the same order) carries science and pedagogy over (`reviews.py` category `reflow`); the visual reviewer always looks again.
- Known limitation: a Digabi editor opened on a wide window and then narrowed under ~400 px can widen the page by ~35 px; `test_writepad.py` checks the phone way (blocking).

## Teacher's read-through of FY3 and FY1 (2026-11-13; every lesson, task, Sovella set and exam; `docs/decisions/lapikaynti-2026-11-13.md`, `docs/audits/fy3-silmailu-2026-11-13.md`)
Where a rule needs a shared fix that is not merged yet, write new content to the rule anyway and never imitate the old form.
- **Start from the basic principle and derive**: a balance calculation starts from its basic equation ($Q_\text{luovutettu} = Q_\text{vastaanotettu}$, $\Sigma \overline{F} = \overline{0}$), then the basic laws ($\eta = E_\text{hyöty}/E$, $E = Pt$, $Q = rm$, $Q = cm\Delta T$), then the combined formula; never open with the combined equation (text, worked examples, the Kaava step of every model solution).
- **Simplify symbolically to the end before substituting**; substitute into the last symbolic form.
- **Every chain shows what it computes**: its first row starts with the quantity, never a bare numeric expression.
- **One equation on one row when it fits; equals signs aligned**; only a phone wraps (model: fy3-7's worked example, stage 5). Never split by hand.
- **One thing per paragraph, in worked-example stages too**: knowns, a remark and the question each on their own (the question last); no paragraph over about 120 words.
- **Figures, photos and small tables beside what they explain** on a wide screen; two small tables side by side; no empty right column or tall empty gap.
- **Nothing the reader does not need**: no reminder rows between stages, no provenance badge on example or simulated data (a real source stays as a small source line), no general study advice on a chapter page.
- **Closed boxes for what is read on demand**: Yleinen virhekäsitys and Tiivistelmä; optional extra investigations inside Lisää tutkittavaa.
- **Compact controls**: an experiment's Alkutila / Vakiona / Muutetaan each on its own row; its buttons on one row; the self-assessment levels in their colours on one row.
- **Pohdinta's retrieval task belongs to its phenomenon**: the "Kertaa yläkoulusta" choice asks the prior knowledge through the same situation; order: phenomenon, retrieval task, open question.
- **A table when the content is a table** (parallel items with the same attributes).
- **Long words break at a syllable with a hyphen**, never mid-syllable.
- **Animation only where it shows what a still cannot**, pausable, still under `prefers-reduced-motion` and in print.

## Exercise levels, lesson folder, assets (full text `docs/decisions/tehtavapankki-ja-simulaatiot.md`)
- **Levels**: 1 perustehtävät (one formula or step, values given), 2 soveltavat (two steps, a conversion, a rearrangement or measured data), 3 haastavat (reasoning, two ideas, modelling, data interpretation, an unfamiliar context, YO-style). None repeats the worked example. Counts are minimums (`curriculum.json` `exercise_levels` default 5/4/3, `exercise_total`); **the exercise bank** (teacher 2026-11-04) gives every FY1–FY8 chapter at least 20 exercises (about 8 + 7 + 5), mixing calculations with conceptual multiple choice, justified qualitative answers, experimental design, error analysis, graph reading and 2–3 **aineistotehtävää** (`"kind"`, `courses.EXERCISE_KINDS`; `python scripts/exercise_bank.py <COURSE>`; plan `docs/exercise-bank/`). Existing exercises, ids and solutions are kept; new ones take the next free ids.
- **Folder** `content/fi/<course>/<id>/`: `meta.json`, `lesson.md`, `inquiry.json`, `exit-ticket.json`, `review/` (science.md, pedagogy.md, visual.md, carry.tsv, developer.md, checks.txt, usage.tsv; packets and shots git-ignored), `exercises/` (`exercises.json`, `solutions.md`, own `review/`; reviewed apart from the lesson). `lessons/<course>/<id>/`, the same for every language: `answers.py`, `lesson-answers.py`, `figures.py` (`scripts/figures.py` writes them into lesson.md), `sim-spec.md`, FY3's `sim.js`.
- **Simulations** live in `assets/sims/<course>/<name>/` (`window.simState`, `defineSimAdapter()`); every text a simulation shows is in `lang/fi.json` under `sims.<name>`, never a literal in sim.js; pictures in `assets/pics/<course>/` as `pics/<course>/<file>`; `assets/shared/` only for what several courses use. A sim with no motion of its own is listed in `STATIC` in `templates/sim-embed.js` (never by a flag in its sim.js); `test_sims.py` checks it.

## Exam readiness (teacher's brief 2026-10-28, NOTES.md §9; a guiding principle for every course)
- **Inquiry stays the foundation.** Exam-style questions are not spread into every stage. Progression is visible at module level: understanding → guided application (Sovella, exercises 1–2) → independent application (level 3) → exam-style transfer (Sovella itsenäisesti).
- **Self-assessment**: no chapter-level "Osaan / Tarvitsen vielä harjoitusta" checklist. Written answers are self-assessed against the model answer's observable criteria, then "what is missing" and where to practise (NOTES.md §11); multiple-choice checks are graded by the page; the point-based rubric stays in Sovella itsenäisesti.
- **Figures** (NOTES.md §11): find an existing figure in `docs/figures/catalog.json` (`python scripts/figcat.py find …`) before drawing one; a task shows a figure by its id (`"figure": "fy1-4/scatter-points"`), never a pasted SVG; a variant without the answer is drawn from the same data in the owner's figures.py.
- **Sovella itsenäisesti**: each course ends with original module-end tasks, as many as its goals need (never three by default), written once per course with `/practice <COURSE>` (`content/<lang>/<COURSE>/sovella/`): authentic situation and source material, parts with points, nothing that names the model, then behind one button a complete worked solution, common mistakes and the shared rubric. Never relabel chapter exercises; prefer replacing a weak activity over adding one.
- **Esimerkkikoe**: after a course is complete, `/exam <COURSE>` writes its example exam from `docs/exam-guidelines.md`. Teacher-written reference exams are private: never in the repository, a packet, a review, a commit or memory, and never paraphrased. Never copy exam questions from YTL or textbooks.
- **Competencies** (`competencies.json`): concept, math, graph, experiment, communication, transfer; never quotas, never off-curriculum. `scripts/coverage.py <COURSE>` writes `docs/coverage/<COURSE>.md`; a gap is closed by a better activity, not by tagging.
- **Token economy**: generate once, reuse. Shared rubric criteria by key, one rubric component, deterministic checks (`practice.py check`, `test_practice.py`, `coverage.py --check`) before any review, no new agents, no model at runtime, translate only new or changed text.

## Pipeline, reviews and CI (full text `docs/decisions/katselmointi-ja-ci.md`)
- **developer** writes and fixes; **science-reviewer**, **pedagogy-reviewer** and **visual-editorial-reviewer** judge independently, each from a packet file (`scripts/review_packet.py`). `docs/EDITORIAL_QUALITY_STANDARD.md` is the shared yardstick. Commands: `/lesson <id>` (`.claude/commands/lesson.md`: routing, budgets, steps), `/exercises`, `/practice`, `/exam`, `/figure-fixes`, `/quality-audit`, `/new-course`. `python scripts/round.py <id>` is the one deterministic round; `python scripts/check_lesson.py <id>` runs every deterministic check before any review, the translation check (`check_i18n.py --lesson <id>`) included.
- **Physics accuracy**: `docs/physics-guardrails.md` is the physics reference every developer and science-reviewer packet carries; `scripts/physics_phrases.py` and `test_physics_phrases.py` keep known wrong claims out; `scripts/concepts.py` gathers one concept's sentences for a cross-chapter science review.
- **The rule** (`scripts/reviews.py`): a lesson is on the site when all three reviews say PASS for its current content hash. Content published before the visual reviewer existed stays under the hash-bound visual baseline (`docs/visual-baseline.tsv`, never added to by hand) until its first material change. The exercise set has its own hash and three reviews (`exercises/review/`, `--exercises`). The hash covers the lesson files, its simulation folder, pictures and simulation tests; formatting, build scripts, templates and configuration never stale a review. What changed decides who looks again (`reviews.py scope`: none, delta or full per reviewer); a PASS that holds over a change is recorded in `review/carry.tsv` (`reviews.py carry`, `carry-item`) and re-derived by `verify` in CI. Carry categories: `teacher-figure` (figures the teacher approved, `approve-figures`, `docs/teacher-figures.tsv`; later change needs new approval), `teacher-text` (a text change the teacher read and approved, `approve-text <id> [--lang] --source "…"`, `docs/teacher-text.tsv`: the exact file before and after; only on the teacher's explicit word, any later edit needs a new approval), `reflow`, `md-paragraphs`, `caption-label`, `formula-names`, `mafy-symbols`, `objective-form`, `presentation-only` (simulation change that only places or draws; `docs/presentation-only.tsv`, `approve-presentation`; anything else in a `sim.js` is `simulation`: full science and pedagogy reviews). No status fields, nothing edited by hand; `review/legacy.md` marks a prototype published before this pipeline.
