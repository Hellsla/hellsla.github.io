FY9 · School-specific course

Physics Matriculation Examination Review

Review, connect and practise for the exam.

This is a school-specific course (Kiviruukki upper secondary school). Its goals and contents come from the school's own plan, not from the national modules FY1–FY8.

Under construction. Units are published as they are completed and checked. The scope and assessment have not been confirmed.

The course reviews the key phenomena, laws and skills of modules FY1–FY8 and practises the task types of the matriculation examination. Every review lesson starts with a starting-level test and ends with original tasks in the style of the matriculation examination, with a model answer and a scoring guide for self-assessment. Each task says which chapter's skills it uses, so you can go straight back to the original chapter to review a weak spot.

In this course, you will learn to

Working version: the goals will be refined when the course plan is complete.

  • recognise the phenomena in a task and choose a suitable physical model for them
  • write a complete solution with reasoning, units and a check that the result makes sense
  • draw and interpret graphs from measurement data
  • plan an experiment and assess how reliable the measurements are
  • combine ideas from different modules in a new situation
  • assess your own skills and review your weak spots
