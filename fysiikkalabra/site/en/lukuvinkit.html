Physics beyond the textbook

Book tips

Physics is not only formulas and calculations. It is also stories, surprising questions, experiments and attempts to understand the world.

In these books you can find new views of familiar phenomena, or meet physics there is no time for in lessons. You do not have to read a book from cover to cover. You can start with one chapter that interests you.

If physics interests you, these books are worth a look. The most approachable ones come first and the most demanding ones last. They cover experiments, everyday phenomena, the history of physics, thought experiments and some rather unusual questions.

Borrow from Lauri The books marked “Borrow from Lauri” are on my own shelf, so feel free to ask to borrow them!

  1. Entäs jos… / What If?

    Randall Munroe

    Borrow from Lauri

    What would happen if everyone in the world jumped at the same time? Absurd questions lead to surprisingly serious physics and fun calculations.

    Language
    Finnish / English
    Level
    easy
    Especially for
    FY1–FY4

  2. What If? 2: Additional Serious Scientific Answers to Absurd Hypothetical Questions

    Randall Munroe

    Borrow from Lauri

    More imaginative questions with scientific answers. Could you cool the atmosphere by opening every freezer at once? The book shows how physics can be used to investigate almost any question.

    Language
    English
    Level
    easy
    Especially for
    FY1–FY4

  3. Laskette varmaankin leikkiä, Mr Feynman!

    Richard P. Feynman

    Humorous and sometimes astonishing memoirs of a Nobel-winning physicist. Above all, the book is about curiosity, experimenting and the way a scientist thinks.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    easy
    Especially for
    FY1, FY10

  4. Tiede – luovaa leikkiä

    Hans Jürgen Press

    200 small experiments in natural science and technology. The book shows that interesting science can be done with simple equipment. A particularly good source of inspiration for your own experiments and research projects.

    Language
    Finnish (Finnish edition only listed here)
    Level
    easy
    Especially for
    FY1, FY3, FY4, FY10

  5. Miksi paahtoleipä putoaa voipuoli alaspäin

    Robert Ehrlich

    Why does toast land butter-side down? And how can everyday household objects show how the laws of physics work? The book presents over a hundred ingenious demonstrations that let you investigate physical phenomena yourself.

    Language
    Finnish (translation; English original: Why Toast Lands Jelly-Side Down)
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY3, FY4, FY10

  6. Herra Tompkins fysiikan ihmemaassa

    George Gamow

    Borrow from Lauri

    A classic from the history of science: What would happen if the speed of light were as slow as the speed of cars? Imaginative adventures make the strange effects of relativity and quantum physics understandable.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY4, FY8

  7. Seitsemän lyhyttä luentoa fysiikasta

    Carlo Rovelli

    Borrow from Lauri

    A short, thought-provoking book on the big ideas of modern physics: relativity, quantum physics and the universe.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8

  8. The Physics of Superheroes

    James Kakalios

    Borrow from Lauri

    Could Spider-Man really swing from his web? How fast could the Flash run? And how do Superman's powers square with Newton's laws? The book examines superheroes' abilities with real physics.

    Language
    English
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY2–FY5, FY7

  9. Storm in a Teacup: The Physics of Everyday Life

    Helen Czerski

    Why does popcorn pop? Why does drying coffee leave a ring? Everyday phenomena open connections to the big principles of physics.

    Language
    English
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1–FY4

  10. Oleta pyöreä lehmä

    Lawrence M. Krauss

    Why might a physicist assume a cow is a sphere? The book opens up the way physicists think and shows how simple models are built from complicated phenomena. A good choice if you want to understand why physics relies on assumptions and estimates.

    Language
    Finnish (translation; English original: Fear of Physics)
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY4, FY8

  11. Stephen Hawking vasta-alkaville ja edistyville

    J. P. McEvoy ja Oscar Zarate

    An illustrated introduction to the life and research of Stephen Hawking. Black holes, relativity and the big questions about the universe become familiar through text and drawings. An alternative if you want to approach Hawking's ideas more lightly than through his own books.

    Language
    Finnish (Finnish edition only listed here)
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8

  12. Fysiikan sankareita viideltä vuosisadalta

    Heikki Oja

    Stories of the people who shaped physics, from Pascal to Higgs. A good choice if you want to know how ideas in physics have changed.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8, FY14

  13. Kuolleiden kanssa kassajonossa – Tieteellistä aikamatkailua

    Leena Tähtinen

    Borrow from Lauri

    Could you travel into the past? Or into the future? The book explores time and the possibilities of time travel from the point of view of physics.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8

  14. The Ten Most Beautiful Experiments

    George Johnson

    Ten landmark experiments in the history of science. The book shows how a simple but cleverly designed experiment can lead to a great scientific discovery.

    Language
    English
    Level
    intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY10

  15. The Flying Circus of Physics

    Jearl Walker

    A collection of strange, fun and sometimes dangerous-looking phenomena of physics. The book invites you to ask questions and look for explanations.

    Language
    English
    Level
    intermediate
    Especially for
    FY3–FY5, FY10

  16. Pimeän aineen arvoitus

    Borrow from Lauri

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8

  17. Maailmankaikkeuden mittaaminen

    Kitty Ferguson

    How was the size of the Earth found out? And how can we measure the distance to a star we cannot travel to? The book tells how, over the centuries, people have learned to measure an ever larger universe.

    Language
    Finnish (Finnish edition only listed here)
    Level
    intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8, FY10

  18. Ajan lyhyt historia

    Stephen Hawking

    Borrow from Lauri

    A classic from the history of science: Where did the universe come from? What are black holes? Does time have a beginning or an end? A well-known classic of modern cosmology.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    intermediate–demanding
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8

  19. Sattuma ja kaaos

    David Ruelle

    Can something be impossible to predict even though it follows exact laws of nature? Chaos theory shows why small differences at the start can lead to big changes. The book challenges you to think about predictability, chance and the laws of nature in a new way.

    Language
    Finnish (Finnish edition only listed here)
    Level
    demanding
    Especially for
    FY4, FY5, FY10

  20. Thirty Years That Shook Physics: The Story of Quantum Theory

    George Gamow

    Borrow from Lauri

    A classic from the history of science: The quantum revolution of the first decades of the 20th century, told by a physicist of that era. Planck, Bohr, Heisenberg, Schrödinger and the other founders of the theory all appear.

    Language
    English
    Level
    demanding
    Especially for
    FY8, FY14
