Lesson 2 · Explore and try

Ahjo's own instruction card

Working safely

The general space induction, the Makerspace licence and device induction for the 3D printer and hand tools; protective equipment and an orderly workstation.

In this lesson you learn: You know Ahjo's rules and how the space induction, the Makerspace licence and a device permit differ.

Start right away (5–10 min)

Find five things in the space that have to do with safety, such as the fire extinguisher, the first aid kit and safety glasses, and photograph them.

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Take the device induction for the 3D printer and the hand tools. The induction does not yet allow you to use a device on your own: the teacher tells you what you may do.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Make an illustrated instruction card for one device in your own style, for example as a drawing or a comic.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Write a risk assessment for the device: the hazard, how likely it is, the consequence and how to protect yourself.

What did you make?

Basic level: The device induction done and an illustrated instruction card for one device.

Challenge yourself: A risk assessment of the device.

You need

  • phone or camera
  • Ahjo's safety instructions
  • safety glasses

Safety
