Periodic motion and waves

Why do objects rotate and oscillate, and how do waves travel?

9 lessons available · 8 more coming

Lessons

  1. Chapter 1Moment of a force and rotation of a body

    Why does a stuck wheel nut come loose with a long spanner, when the same force on a short spanner cannot make it budge?

  2. Chapter 2Rotational equilibrium

    Why can a paint can be placed on the overhang of a plank lying across trestles, but only up to a certain point before the plank topples?

  3. Chapter 3Circular motion and speed

    Why does a child standing at the edge of a roundabout rush along much faster than a child standing next to the centre pole, even though both complete a turn equally fast?

  4. Chapter 4Uniform circular motion and centripetal acceleration

    Why does a car slide off the road on a slippery bend?

  5. Chapter 5Newton's law of gravitation

    Why does a dropped apple rush towards the Earth, while the Earth does not seem to move towards the apple at all?

  6. Chapter 6Circular orbits under gravity

    Why does the International Space Station ISS not fall to the Earth, and why do the astronauts float inside it?

  7. Chapter 7Periodic motion

    Why can the motion of a child on a swing be described with a few numbers, even though the swing is in a different place at every moment?

  8. Chapter 8Harmonic force and elastic potential energy

    Why does a pinball launcher shoot the ball faster the further its spring is compressed?

  9. Chapter 9Simple harmonic motion and the pendulum

    Why does every swing, pendulum and body bobbing on a spring oscillate at its own pace, and what determines that pace?

Module goals

Show all 4 goals

The student

  • can model planetary motion as circular motion
  • becomes familiar with the basics of oscillation and wave motion by investigating mechanical oscillation and sound
  • can describe periodic motion with physical and mathematical concepts
  • can model mechanical oscillation and sound as periodic motion
