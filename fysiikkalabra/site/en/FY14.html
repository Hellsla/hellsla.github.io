Road to Fusion

From the Birth of Modern Physics to ITER.

This is a school-specific course (Kiviruukki upper secondary school). Its goals and contents come from the school's own plan, not from the national modules FY1–FY8.

Under construction. Units are published as they are completed and checked. The scope and assessment have not been confirmed.

Authors: Lauri Hellsten and Marjo Inkala

An interdisciplinary course taught in English that combines physics, English and French (CLIL). It travels from the birth of modern physics through the energy of the nucleus, fusion in the stars and plasma to the Cold War race for fusion and the international ITER project. The whole course answers one question: if scientists have understood nuclear fusion for almost a century, why is producing electricity from fusion still so difficult?

In this course, you will learn to

Working version: the goals will be refined when the course plan is complete.

  • explain why classical physics was not enough and how quantum physics was born
  • calculate the energy of nuclear reactions from the mass defect and binding energy
  • explain why fusion needs extreme temperatures and how magnetic fields confine a plasma
  • evaluate how politics, cooperation and technology have shaped fusion research
  • distinguish verified research results from predictions and commercial claims
  • discuss and write about science in English and get by in everyday situations in French
