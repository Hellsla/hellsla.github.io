Lesson 1 · Get to know drones and fly safely

Flight area rules and roles

Getting to know drones and flight safety

The parts of a drone, marking out the flight area and three roles: pilot, observer and safety officer.

In this lesson you learn: You know the main parts of a drone and can act in your own role in the flight area.

Needs the teacher's safety approval

Start right away (5–10 min)

Watch the drone flown by the teacher and write down three things that could go wrong. Compare your list with a partner.

Dronelabra safety induction →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    With your group, make a pre-flight checklist and draw the classroom flight area: the take-off spot, the safety line and the place for spectators. Practise the roles and the calls Take-off, Stop and Land without a drone.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Add an item to the checklist for each hazard you wrote down at the start, and test the list with another group.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Find out from Traficom's Unmanned aviation (Miehittämätön ilmailu) pages what is required of someone flying a camera drone that weighs less than 250 grams. Write down three rules and the page where you found them.

What did you make?

Basic level: The group's checklist and a drawing of the flight area, which are used throughout the course.

Challenge yourself: Three rules of flying in your own words, with sources.

Write in your project diary

  • the group's checklist
  • the drawing of the flight area and the roles
  • one thing that surprised you

Together

Swap checklists with another group. Does their list have an item that your own group did not notice?

You need

  • paper and pens
  • masking tape for marking out the flight area
  • safety glasses for the safety officer
  • a drone flown by the teacher

Step-by-step guide

  • Dronelabra safety induction

Safety

In this lesson only the teacher flies the drone. Stay behind the safety line when the propellers are spinning.

  • Drones: propellers and motors
  • Drones: flight area and roles
  • Drones: rules indoors and outdoors

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Start and demonstration flight 15 min, checklist and flight area 30 min, practising the roles 20 min, summary 10 min.

Preparation: Book a room with free floor space and agree on the use of the room with the school. Charge the batteries and check the drone. Print the safety induction guide. No devices are handed out to students in this lesson.
