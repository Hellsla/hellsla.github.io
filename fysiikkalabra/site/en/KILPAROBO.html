Competitive robotics – join us!

Kiviruukki upper secondary school's robotics team takes part in VEX V5 robotics competitions. In the team we design, build, program and develop competition robots together.

You need no earlier robotics experience. More experienced students help new ones get started, and everyone learns by doing. You fit in if you are interested in, for example, building, programming, measuring, game strategy, photography or organising events.

In competitions we try to do well, but the most important things are learning, solving problems and working together. A robot is never finished: it can always be tested, fixed and improved.

How do I join?

  1. Ask the teacher or a team member when the team meets next.
  2. Come to one meeting. You get a mentor, a more experienced student, who shows you the robot and the workspace.
  3. Complete the safety induction before you use tools or drive the robot.
  4. Start from the first stage of the beginner's path and go at your own pace.

You do not need to have taken Ahjo 1 or Ahjo 2. You can join straight away.

How the team works

The team is student-led. Students agree together what to do to the robot next and share the work. The teacher is responsible for the activity and safety and supports when needed.

  • Mechanics and building

    Chassis, drivetrain, grippers and other mechanisms. Design, build and repair.

  • Programming and automation

    Driver control, autonomous programs and sensors in the VEXcode V5 programming environment.

  • Testing and measuring

    Find out by measuring whether a change helped: time, distance, accuracy and repeatability.

  • Competition strategy and driver practice

    Analysing the game's rules and scoring, a match plan and driving practice.

  • Documentation, communication and competition arrangements

    The development diary, photos, presenting the team and preparing competition trips with the teacher.

  • Roles are not fixed. You can try different tasks and change area during the season.
  • Everyone takes part in developing the robot as far as they can.
  • Experienced students do not do the tasks for new ones. They guide, ask and give feedback.
  • Skills stay in the team: record what you do so that next year's students can carry on from there.

Ahjo 1, Ahjo 2 and competitive robotics

Ahjo's courses and competitive robotics support each other. In Ahjo you practise basic technology skills and project work; in competitive robotics you apply them to a competition challenge. You do not need either course before joining.

Ahjo 1

  • Basics of technology
  • Measuring
  • Building
  • First steps in programming

Ahjo 2

  • Project planning
  • Prototyping
  • Testing
  • Iteration

Competitive robotics

  • Applying skills to a competition challenge
  • Teamwork
  • Competition strategy
  • Long-term robot development
  • Mentoring

Ahjo's robotics tasks use Arduino. Competitive robotics uses the VEX V5 system, whose parts and programming are different.
