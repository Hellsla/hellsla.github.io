Lesson 9 · FPV, measuring and data

First FPV flight

The basics of FPV flying

You fly a camera drone with goggles and a motion controller in Normal mode. The observer stands next to the pilot.

In this lesson you learn: You fly an FPV drone under control and act as observer.

Needs the teacher's safety approval

Start right away (5–10 min)

Hold the motion controller in your hand with the device switched off and practise the movements the teacher shows: braking and hovering, climbing, turning and landing.

FPV flight with goggles and a motion controller →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    At the station, do one FPV flight: take-off, hover, a slow flight to the marker and back, and landing. Also act once as observer and once as safety officer.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Fly the same route without goggles, looking straight at the drone. Which way was more accurate, and why?

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Describe what information the view in the goggles gives the pilot and what information only the observer sees.

If you have to wait

When it is not your turn to fly, practise the control movements with the controller switched off, or plan the precision course for next time.

What did you make?

Basic level: One controlled FPV flight and experience of each of the three roles.

Challenge yourself: A comparison between FPV flight and direct line of sight.

Write in your project diary

  • the roles you acted in
  • how FPV flight felt compared with direct line of sight
  • the observer's observations

Together

After the flight, the observer tells the pilot one thing that the pilot did not see through the goggles.

You need

  • a camera drone with propeller guards
  • FPV goggles and a motion controller
  • charged batteries
  • marker cones or tape markers
  • safety glasses for the safety officer

Safety

With the goggles on you cannot see around you. The observer stands next to you, keeps the drone in sight the whole time without any aids and says Stop if anything comes into the flight area. You fly only in Normal mode. Stop immediately if you start to feel dizzy or unwell.

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Control movements and rules 15 min, stations 50 min, summary 10 min. With two drones, a group of about four students per station has time to fly.

Preparation: Read the device's user manual and safety instructions. The manufacturer states that the product is not intended for children and does not give an age limit: the school decides on its use in teaching. Check who the registered user (operator) is and that the ID is marked on the drones. Agree on the use of the room and on the rules for filming. Attach the propeller guards, charge the batteries and make sure that the flying space is well lit and roomy: in a cramped or dim space the drone can lose its positioning and drift. Sport mode and Easy ACRO tricks are not used, because obstacle avoidance is switched off in them. Clean the goggles between users. Arrange a parallel task for the rest of the group.
