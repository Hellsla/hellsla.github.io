Lesson 7 · Flight tests and flight stability

First hover

First flight tests with the Air:bit 2

Checklist, calibration and short hovers in a marked-out flight area.

In this lesson you learn: You carry out the pre-flight checks and fly the drone briefly and under control.

Needs the teacher's safety approval

Start right away (5–10 min)

Go through the group's checklist aloud and share out the roles: pilot, safety officer and recorder. Show the list to the teacher before the propellers are attached.

Flight tests and measuring →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Attach the propellers under the teacher's supervision and let the drone calibrate while standing still on a level surface. Do three short hovers: raise the throttle one step at a time until the drone hovers at a height of 10–30 cm. For each flight, record the duration and the direction in which the drone drifted.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Swap roles and repeat the test. Did the direction of the drift change when the pilot changed or when the drone was calibrated again?

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Plan a test to find out whether the drift is caused by the drone or by the pilot. Write down what you keep constant and what you change.

If you have to wait

While another group is flying, act as recorder or measure the duration of their hover from video.

What did you make?

Basic level: A flight log of three hovers: duration, drift and observations.

Challenge yourself: A test plan for finding the cause of the drift.

Write in your project diary

  • the flight log
  • which checklist item was the most important
  • what you would change next time

Together

Give the pilot one compliment and one piece of advice after each flight.

You need

  • an assembled and tested Air:bit 2
  • a micro:bit programmed as the controller
  • charged batteries
  • propellers and propeller guards
  • safety glasses
  • stopwatch
  • tape measure
  • masking tape
  • a soft surface, for example a rug, on the floor of the flight area

Step-by-step guide

Starter files

  • Flight log (CSV) template for a spreadsheet

Safety

The drone has no altitude hold or position hold: the pilot controls the height the whole time. You fly low over a soft surface, one drone at a time in the flight area. The emergency stop is shaking the controller, and it is tried out before the propellers are attached.

  • Drones: propellers and motors
  • Drones: charging and storing batteries
  • Drones: flight area and roles
  • Drones: emergency stop and abnormal situations

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Checklist and roles 10 min, propellers and calibration 15 min, flying turns 40 min, summary 10 min.

Preparation: Check the flying space and mark out the flight area. Charge the batteries and have spare propellers ready. Decide how long the flying turns are and how many drones are running at once. Approve each group's checklist before the flight.
