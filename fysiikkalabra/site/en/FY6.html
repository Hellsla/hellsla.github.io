Electricity

How does electric current flow in a circuit and transfer energy?

9 lessons available · 7 more coming

Lessons

Direct-current circuits

  1. Chapter 1Electric circuit, current and voltage

    Why does the lamp of a torch light at the same moment as you press the switch, although the electrons in the wire move more slowly than a snail?

  2. Chapter 2Measurements in an electric circuit

    Why is an ammeter connected to a circuit in a different way from a voltmeter, and what happens if the connections get mixed up?

  3. Chapter 3Resistance and Ohm's law

    Why is the heating wire of a toaster made of thin special metal, while the conductors of an extension cord are thick copper?

  4. Chapter 4Resistors in series and in parallel

    Why do all the bulbs of an old string of fairy lights go out when one bulb breaks, while at home the floor lamp stays on even when the desk lamp is switched off?

  5. Chapter 5Kirchhoff's laws and potential in a circuit

    Why can a bird sit on a bare high-voltage wire unharmed, even though a current of hundreds of amperes flows in the wire?

Electrical energy and sources

  1. Chapter 6Electric power and the heating of a resistor

    Why do the resistance wires of a toaster glow red while the toaster's cord stays cool, even though the same current flows through both?

  2. Chapter 7Electrical energy at home

    Why is an electricity bill calculated from kilowatt-hours and not from the watts of the home's devices?

  3. Chapter 8Electromotive force and internal resistance

    Why do the headlights of a car dim for a moment when the engine is started on a cold winter morning?

  4. Chapter 9Batteries and the charging circuit

    Why does a phone battery charge quickly at the start, but the last few percent take a long time?

  • knows safety aspects related to electrical devices and the transmission of electrical energy
