## Safety at the stations

- High voltages: the electron tube (up to 300 V), the oil-drop plates (about 500 V), the gas tubes. Change connections only when the instructor allows it.
- The electron tube is thin glass: a magnet may come near it, but never touch it.
- Lamps and gas tubes get hot. Do not touch them. Do not look straight into a bright lamp or LED from close by.
- The alpha source of the oil-drop station stays inside the apparatus. Only the instructor handles it.
- Do not run the filament lamp at 10 V for long.

## Useful equations

- Photoelectric effect: e·Uₛ = h·f − W, so Uₛ = (h/e)·f − W/e. Slope of Uₛ against f = h/e. f = c/λ.
- Electron beam: 2U = (e/m)·B²r². Coil pair: B = (4/5)^(3/2)·μ₀·N·I/R, μ₀ = 4π·10⁻⁷ T·m/A.
- Double slit: bright bands Δy = λ·L/d apart (small angles).
- Wien's law: λ_max·T = 2.898·10⁻³ m·K.
- Repeated readings: the mean; Δx of one reading ≈ (largest − smallest)/2, that is, half of the range (it shows how much single readings spread).
