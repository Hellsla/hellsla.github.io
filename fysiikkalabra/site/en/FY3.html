Module goals

Show all 4 goals

The student

  • deepens their understanding of energy as a central concept of physics
  • can investigate phenomena related to the thermodynamic state of matter and to changes of state
  • can apply the concepts and models of thermodynamics when examining energy production solutions and building a sustainable future
  • recognises the importance of energy balance and heat transfer in climate change
