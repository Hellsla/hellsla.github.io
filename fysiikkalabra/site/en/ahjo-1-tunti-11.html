Lesson 11 · Electronics and Arduino

A moving figure

Motors and actuators

Servo and DC motor; power supply and control.

In this lesson you learn: You control a servo with a program and a potentiometer.

Start right away (5–10 min)

Connect a servo and a potentiometer as in the wiring picture and open the ready example Knob in the Arduino IDE. Turn the knob: the servo follows.

Servo and potentiometer →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Program the servo to turn to three different angles.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Build a cardboard figure on the servo that waves, nods or points.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Make the servo follow the light, steered by two light-dependent resistors.

What did you make?

Basic level: Working servo control and your own moving figure or mechanism.

Challenge yourself: A light-following servo.

Together

Develop another pair's figure further: add one movement and note in your project diary whose work you built on.

You need

  • Arduino Uno and USB cable
  • servo and potentiometer
  • card and tape for the figure

Step-by-step guide

  • Servo and potentiometer
  • Force on a current-carrying wire: the electric motor and the loudspeaker
  • Small extra challenge: A bristle robot

Safety

  • Soldering and electronics work

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.
