## Lesson page (human decisions 2026-10-26, every new or edited lesson)
The shared templates (`templates/`, `scripts/build.py`, `scripts/inquiry.py`) do 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8 for every lesson; the
lesson does 6, 9 and its half of 4. `lint_lesson.py` checks 4, 6 and 9 as errors on a lesson being written or edited
(warnings on one reviewed before 2026-10-26), `test_inquiry.py` and `check_layout.py` check the page at 360, 390, 640 (200 % zoom), 768, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px, closed and with its hints, solutions and feedback open (teacher 2026-10-26: the layout must work at every width).
1. **One reading column; width only where two things belong together** (teacher 2026-10-27, "luettavuus ja taitto"). Text and tasks keep a 38rem column (never over 44rem) at every width. Tasks are never side by side: a card grows when its feedback, hints or solution opens and must not leave an empty column beside it. The one exception is the student's own choice (teacher 2026-11-05, not the default): from 1100 px "Avaa tehtävät teorian viereen" (or the Tehtävät link) opens the exercises as a panel on the right that scrolls on its own, the lesson on the left (`lesson.js` part 9, `html.tasks-split` in site.css, checked in test_inquiry.py). Side by side only: the illustration beside "Tällä tunnilla opit", a figure beside the paragraph it explains when the two are about as tall (else under it), the simulation beside the experiments (sticky). The worked example's steps run under its problem in the reading column (teacher 2026-11-03).
   Design system (`templates/site.css` header): three levels — the stage (numbered 24 px heading on a full-width rule), the task (one card: heading, situation, question, answers, check, feedback or solution), what sits inside a task (tinted panels); one type scale (title 32–36 px Fraunces, stage 24, task 19, text 18 on a 1.6 line, inside a task 17, interface 15, meta 14 px, Plus Jakarta Sans); the simulations keep 17 px on a 1.65 line (their label metrics depend on it); the stage navigation is the progress bar (teacher 2026-11-03): a sticky chip bar under the top edge at every width (under 700 px one button, "3/7 Tutki", that opens the whole list; teacher 2026-11-10, below), the stage on screen marked, a stage ticked once its required answers are given (kept in localStorage `ruukkilabra:<page>`, `lesson.js` part 2, `test_progress.py`). Fix a layout problem in the shared components, never per lesson.
2. **Pohdinta**, not "Herätä kiinnostus", as the first stage's heading and in the navigation.
3. **Ennusta shows its first prediction at once** (teacher 2026-11-13, work queue 8h: "ensimmäinen ennuste pitäisi aina näkyä"): the first required prediction question stands open at the start of the stage, no box to open; the others, if any, wait in one closed box (`<details>`, keyboard operable) titled "Lisää ennusteita · N kysymystä" (English "More predictions · N questions"; teacher 2026-11-14, "Joo"; N the questions in the box); a lesson with one prediction has no box at all. The structure comes from `inquiry.py`, so it works without JavaScript; commitment before the answer, storing, locking and Palaa ennusteisiisi are unchanged, and the box can be reopened later and closing it keeps the answers and reasons. An optional prediction that comes before the first required one goes into the box.
4. **Little text in the simulation.** The picture, the controls and the key quantities and values show; usage, the model's assumptions and the computation sit behind closed boxes named Käyttöohje, Mallin oletukset and Laskennan selitys (the sim activity's `howto`, `model`, `calc` in inquiry.json; its visible `text` is a lead of at most 30 words). Never hide a control the student needs; sim.js shows at most one legend line.
5. **Teoria**, not "Selitä" or "Teoriatiivistelmä", as the theory stage's heading and in the navigation; the stage after it is **Esimerkit** (the worked example, then practice with hints), not "Sovella", which names only the module's *Sovella itsenäisesti* (teacher 2026-11-05). Esimerkit opens straight with the worked example: no stage has a lead line since 2026-11-14 (teacher; Testaa ja arvioi lost it 2026-11-13; the build still shows a `inquiry.lead_<stage>` text if a language defines one). Since 2026-11-06 (teacher): the last stage is **Itsearvio** (was Tarkista osaamisesi; id `tarkista`), its exit ticket **Pohdintatehtävä** and its checks named by kind, *Käsitetehtävä*, *Laskutehtävä*, *Selitystehtävä* (`inquiry.task_titles`); every box of Esimerkit is a guided example, "Esimerkki 1: <name>", the worked example's short name in `example-names.json` (outside the lessons, so no review goes stale; a new lesson adds its name there); Palaa ennusteisiisi and Kertaa luvun käsitteet are closed boxes. Older texts that say Teoriatiivistelmä / Theory summary are shown with the new name (`inquiry.current_stage_names`), so their reviews stand; new text writes Teoria / Theory.
6. **The formula section is named after its law**: `## Kaava: Lämpömäärä` shows "Lämpömäärä" (or Hydrostaattinen paine, Ideaalikaasun tilanyhtälö, by the lesson's content); "Kaava" stays the key that inquiry.json and the scripts use.
   **FY9 formulas carry their names** (teacher 2026-11-12, "FY09 kaavat pitäisi nimetä kuten ne on MAFY taulukossa"): a ```kaavat fence, one formula per line as `name :: LaTeX`, shows each formula with its name in bold beside it (above it on a phone), like the formula tables of mafytaulukot.fi (`scripts/kaavat.py`, styles in site.css). `docs/fy9/kaavanimet.tsv` is the approved list: every FY9 formula, its MAFY name (none for a formula not in MAFY; the proposals were taken out on 2026-11-12), the English name; `python scripts/kaavat.py write` puts the names into fi, fi-easy and en. A unit's reviews carry over approved names and nothing else (`reviews.py carry-item unit <id> [--lang]`, category `formula-names`, `test_kaavat.py`); a new name or a changed formula needs the table updated first. **FY1–FY8 one module at a time** (teacher 2026-11-12, "nimeä ainoastaan kaavat jotka ovat mafyssa"): only a formula that is in the MAFY tables gets a name, MAFY's own (no proposals); `docs/<module>/kaavanimet.tsv` (first column `tunti`) lists every display formula of the module's lessons, the same `kaavat.py write` names them, and a lesson's reviews carry over the approved names alone (`reviews.py carry <id> [--lang]`, category `formula-names`). So a student knows that the named formulas are in the MAFY tables and the others are not (teacher 2026-11-12). In a table a formula without a name is a row with an empty name cell; another form of a named formula (ρ = m/V, then m = ρV) is a line `:: m = \rho V` (`=` in the list's name column), shown under its name; a block with no name stays ```latex. Reviews carry over names taken out as well (`kaavat.ANY` for the passed version). FY1 and FY2 keep their own notation (teacher 2026-11-12: FY2 uses E, Q only from FY3; light c = fλ; fy1-5's v = Δs/Δt is named keskivauhti). **FY9 is written as MAFY** (teacher 2026-11-12, "siellä treenataan yo-kokeisiin"): `docs/fy9/mafy-tunnukset.tsv` lists the teacher's exact replacements (W_a/W_o, ℓ, R_kok = ΣR, ΣI_t = ΣI_l, …), `kaavat.py symbols` makes them in the units' texts and figures.py, and a unit's reviews carry over them alone (category `mafy-symbols`; a label box's place and size in a figure follow its formula and are left out, the drawing is judged from figures.py). **Johda itse** (teacher 2026-11-12, "nämä pitäisi kurssikokeessa kuitenkin johtaa"): in FY9 a result the exam asks to derive from MAFY's formulas (napajännite, satelliitin ratanopeus, …) is not a formula of its own: the formula table keeps MAFY's laws, one short sentence states the model, and a ```johda fence (the question, a line `---`, the derivation in paragraphs; `kaavat.johda_html`, styles `.johda` in site.css) shows the question with the derivation behind *Näytä johto*; `scripts/kaavanimet_sync.py <unit>` rewrites the unit's rows of the list after formulas are taken out. The circular-motion acceleration is always *normaalikiihtyvyys* (teacher 2026-11-12, also where MAFY says keskeiskiihtyvyys; glossary.json lists keskeiskiihtyvyys as its other name). FY1, FY2 and FY3 are done; FY4 next.
7. **Exit ticket**, not "Lähtölippu", in every heading and link a student sees. It opens Tarkista osaamisesi, before the checks, and has no step of its own in the navigation (teacher 2026-11-04, "yksi vaihe vähemmän"); its lead is the shared `inquiry.exit_lead`.
8. **Tehtävät last**: the exercises close the page, after Tarkista osaamisesi, with their levels, answer checks and solutions; predictions and the questions of the investigation stay where they are.
9. **Textbook illustrations**: every chapter opens with an illustration of its Miksi question (teacher 2026-10-29, NOTES.md §3 "Opening illustration", checked by the lint); polished inline SVG in the figure palette (NOTES.md §3), recognisable materials, consistent strokes, room around things, labels that read on a phone; colour carries the physics (a temperature rise stands out) but never alone; quantities to scale. `content/fi/FY3/fy3-6` is the model.
