Lesson 8 · Flight tests and flight stability

Stability test

Investigating flight stability and developing the program

How the values of the feedback controller affect how steadily the drone hovers.

In this lesson you learn: You do a comparison test and use measurements to justify whether the flight stability improved.

Needs the teacher's safety approval

Start right away (5–10 min)

Look in last time's flight log to see how much the drone drifted. Set a target for the group: what would a steadier hover mean, and how would you measure it?

Flight tests and measuring →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Do a comparison test with the original tuning values: three hovers, for each of which you measure the drift from the starting point in a set time. Calculate the mean and the range.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    With the teacher's permission, change one tuning value in small steps and repeat the same test. Record the value before and after. Restore the original value if the drone starts to wobble.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Use your observations to explain what the gain of the feedback controller does: why does too small a value make the drone sluggish and too large a value make it oscillate?

If you have to wait

While the drone is with another group, enter your measurements in a table and calculate the means.

What did you make?

Basic level: The results of the comparison test: the mean and range of the drift with the original values.

Challenge yourself: A second series of measurements with a changed value and a justified conclusion.

Write in your project diary

  • the test set-up and what you kept constant
  • the table of measurements and the means
  • the changed values and the original values

Together

Compare your results with another group's. Did you measure the drift in the same way?

You need

  • an Air:bit 2 in flying condition and a controller
  • a computer and MakeCode
  • charged batteries
  • tape measure
  • stopwatch
  • safety glasses
  • a starting point taped on the floor

Step-by-step guide

  • Flight tests and measuring
  • The Air:bit 2 radio controller and control program

Starter files

  • Flight log (CSV) template for a spreadsheet
  • Analysis of series of measurements (Python) untested draft for the graph; the statistics were tried with the example data

Safety

Tuning values are changed one at a time and only within the limits approved by the teacher. A changed program is tested first without propellers.

  • Drones: propellers and motors
  • Drones: charging and storing batteries
  • Drones: flight area and roles
  • Drones: emergency stop and abnormal situations

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Target and test set-up 15 min, comparison test 25 min, change and repeat test 25 min, summary 10 min.

Preparation: Decide in advance which values may be changed and by how much. Save the original program for each group. Charge the batteries: flight time affects the results, so agree that the measurements are made with a full battery. The manufacturer's instructions do not include changing the tuning values, so the task is the school's own: try it yourself before the lesson.
