Lesson 10 · FPV, measuring and data

A series of precision landing measurements

Precision flight test and measuring the flight path

How accurately does a landing hit the mark, and how much do the results vary?

In this lesson you learn: You repeat a measurement and describe the variation in the results with the mean and the range.

Needs the teacher's safety approval

Start right away (5–10 min)

Tape a landing mark on the floor and agree in your group which point the landing accuracy is measured from. Write the rule down before the first flight.

Flight tests and measuring →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Fly from the starting point to the mark and land five times. Measure the distance of each landing from the centre of the mark and record the results in a table.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Change one thing, for example the starting distance or the pilot, and repeat the series of five landings. Keep everything else the same.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Film the flight from the side with a phone on a tripod and measure from the video the flight time from take-off to landing. Calculate the drone's average speed.

If you have to wait

While the drone is with another group, measure and record their landings or enter your own results in the table.

What did you make?

Basic level: A table of measurements for five landings and their mean.

Challenge yourself: A second series of measurements in which one thing has been changed, or the average speed calculated from video.

Write in your project diary

  • the measuring rule and the test set-up
  • the table of measurements
  • the biggest source of error and how you would reduce it

Together

Another group measures one of your landings. Do you get the same result?

You need

  • a camera drone with propeller guards and a controller
  • charged batteries
  • tape measure
  • masking tape
  • a phone and a tripod for filming
  • safety glasses for the safety officer

Step-by-step guide

Starter files

Safety

Only the flight area and the drone are filmed. Nobody is filmed without permission, and the videos are not published.

  • Drones: flight area and roles
  • Drones: emergency stop and abnormal situations
  • Drones: FPV flight and the observer
  • Drones: filming and privacy

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Test set-up and measuring rule 15 min, series of measurements at the stations 45 min, recording the results and summary 15 min.

Preparation: Mark out two flight areas if there is enough room. Have tape measures and a tripod ready. Remind students of the rules for filming. The rest of the group can measure Air:bit hovers or analyse earlier data.
