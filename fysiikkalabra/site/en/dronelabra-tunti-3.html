Lesson 3 · Build and program the Air:bit

Parts map of the drone

The structure and components of the Air:bit 2

Frame, control board, motors, propellers, battery and micro:bit: what each part does.

In this lesson you learn: You name the parts of the drone and say what each of them does.

Works with the present equipment

Start right away (5–10 min)

Open the building kit and sort the parts into groups before you read the instructions. Guess what each group is for.

Assembling the Air:bit 2 and function tests →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    On the control board, identify the micro:bit connector, the motor connections, the battery connector and the charging connector. Draw a parts map that shows where the power and the control signals travel.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Work out from the propellers which way each one has to spin, and mark the directions on the parts map.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Weigh the parts of the kit and calculate the mass of the whole drone. Estimate how large a share of the mass is battery, and think about why that matters.

What did you make?

Basic level: A parts map with the parts of the drone and their jobs labelled.

Challenge yourself: A table of masses and the battery's share of the drone's mass.

Write in your project diary

  • the parts map
  • a question you do not know the answer to yet

Together

Explain the job of one part to your partner so that they can point to it on the parts map.

You need

  • one Air:bit 2 building kit per group
  • micro:bit
  • kitchen scale
  • paper and pens

Step-by-step guide

  • Assembling the Air:bit 2 and function tests

Safety

The battery is not connected yet. Do not bend or press the battery, and tell the teacher straight away if a battery is swollen or damaged.

  • Drones: charging and storing batteries

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Sorting 10 min, identifying the parts and parts map 35 min, propellers and weighing 20 min, summary 10 min.

Preparation: Check that every kit has all its parts, and number the kits and micro:bits by group. Charge the batteries ready for next time according to the manufacturer's instructions.
