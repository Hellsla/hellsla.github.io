Lesson 1 · Explore and try

A torch from found objects

Welcome to Ahjo

The space, the equipment and how we work; the rules and the general induction.

In this lesson you learn: You recognise the parts of a circuit and why an LED works only one way round.

Start right away (5–10 min)

Connect an LED to a coin cell and it lights up. Turn the LED round and see what happens.

Project journal →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Make the LED light and explain why it lights only one way round.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Change how the light looks with a ping-pong ball, paper, foil or a plastic bottle, and pick your favourite.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Walk round Ahjo and record one real problem you would like to solve during the course.

What did you make?

Basic level: A working light in its own cover and the first entry in the project journal.

Challenge yourself: A recorded real problem you would like to solve during the course.

Together

Show your light to the person next to you. Try whose shell spreads the light most evenly, and swap one idea.

You need

  • LED
  • CR2032 coin cell
  • ping-pong ball, paper, foil or a plastic bottle
  • project diary

Step-by-step guide

  • Project journal
  • Small extra challenge: A light-up paper circuit card

Safety

A coin cell is dangerous if swallowed. Keep it on the table, never put it in your mouth and give it back to the teacher at the end of the lesson. Do not join the cell's terminals with metal, as the cell can heat up.
