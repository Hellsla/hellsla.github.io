Lesson 9 · Electronics and Arduino

Your own light rhythm

A first Arduino program

The programming environment, digital outputs and a blinking LED.

In this lesson you learn: You program an Arduino output pin and change the program's timing.

Start right away (5–10 min)

Move the LED circuit's wire from the 5V pin to pin 13, open the ready example Blink in the Arduino IDE and change one delay number. The LED now blinks to your beat.

First steps with Arduino →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Make the LED blink with the example program.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Turn the blinking into a heartbeat, a lighthouse or your own name in Morse code.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Program a reaction-time game with a button the teacher has wired in advance, and work out the mean and spread of the class's results.

What did you make?

Basic level: A working LED program you have changed yourself: your own light rhythm.

Challenge yourself: A reaction-time game and the mean and spread of the class's results.

Together

Show your light rhythm to a partner. Can they tell what it shows?

You need

  • Arduino Uno and USB cable
  • computer and Arduino IDE
  • LED circuit on the breadboard
