Safety checklist

Use: Before every workshop, together with the teacher.

Download as a text file
SAFETY CHECK
============

Workshop: 

Checked together (teacher and tutors): 


IN EVERY WORKSHOP

[ ] The teacher in charge or another responsible adult is there the whole time.
[ ] I know where the first aid kit and the emergency exits are.
[ ] Walkways are clear, and there are no cables or things on the floor.
[ ] The equipment suits the participants' age.
[ ] Participants have been asked about allergies if food, latex or another allergen is used.
[ ] No personal data is collected from participants, and they are not photographed without permission.

ELECTRICITY

[ ] Only batteries or low voltage are used, never mains electricity.
[ ] Button cells are out of children's reach.
[ ] Participants are told not to short-circuit a battery: the wire can get hot.
[ ] Broken, leaking or hot batteries are taken out of use at once.

CHEMICALS AND WATER

[ ] Only substances the teacher has approved. The safety data has been read.
[ ] Safety glasses are worn when a substance can splash.
[ ] Nothing is tasted.
[ ] A wet floor is dried at once.

TOOLS AND LIGHT

[ ] Sharp tools only under guidance, not for young children.
[ ] No laser pointers are used.
[ ] No one looks straight at the sun or a bright lamp.
[ ] Lamps that get hot are placed where no one touches them.

IF SOMETHING HAPPENS

[ ] I stop the activity and call the responsible adult at once.
[ ] If a child may have swallowed a button cell or a magnet, call 112 at once. Do not wait for symptoms.
[ ] I tell the teacher what happened on the same day.

Cards of good guiding questions

Use: Print, cut out the cards and keep them with you in the workshop.

Download as a text file
GUIDING QUESTIONS
=================


SPARK CURIOSITY

- What do you notice?
- What is strange or surprising here?
- What would you like to know about this?

PREDICT

- What do you think will happen if…?
- Why do you think so?
- Could something else be possible?

EXPLORE

- How could you test it?
- What do you change, and what stays the same?
- What happens if you do it differently?

OBSERVE

- What happened?
- Did what you predicted happen?
- Did someone else notice something different?

EXPLAIN

- Why do you think it happened like that?
- How would you explain this to a friend?
- What evidence supports your idea?

APPLY AND REFLECT

- Where in everyday life do you see the same phenomenon?
- What would you do next?
- What was the most surprising thing today?

WHEN SOMEONE IS STUCK

- What have you already tried?
- Which part works and which does not?
- What if we try one change at a time?
