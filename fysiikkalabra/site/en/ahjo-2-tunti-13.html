Lesson 13 · Improve

Final testing

In your project now: Does the solution work: the same measures as in the test plan.

A lesson is a guiding model, not an order that binds every group. If your group is at another stage, agree the next step with the teacher and record in the journal what you actually did.

In this lesson you learn: You assess with the same measures as in the test plan whether you reached the goal.

Next task

Test the final solution with the same measures or observations as in the test plan and judge whether you reached the goal set at the start. If you changed the solution after the previous test, test the changed part again.

Safety

Test your own prototype only with machines and tools you have been inducted into and the teacher has allowed. If the prototype has electricity, moving parts or heat, agree the test with the teacher before you start.
