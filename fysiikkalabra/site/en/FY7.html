How are electricity and magnetism linked, and what is light?

Lessons

Magnetic field and magnetic force

  1. Chapter 1Magnets, the magnetic dipole and the magnetic field

    Why does a compass needle in the forest always point north, but swing away the moment a magnet is brought next to it?

  2. Chapter 2Charged particle in electric and magnetic fields

    Why can a laboratory tell the carbon-14 atoms of an ancient piece of wood from ordinary carbon atoms, although they behave the same chemically and there is only about one in a trillion of them?

  3. Chapter 3Magnetic field of a wire and a coil

    Why can the magnet of a scrapyard crane lift a tonne of scrap car into the air and drop it at the press of a button, when a fridge magnet never lets go?

  4. Chapter 4Force on a current-carrying wire: the electric motor and the loudspeaker

    Why does a loudspeaker cone vibrate and the blades of a desk fan turn, when nothing visible pushes either of them?

Module goals

  • recognises sources and effects of electromagnetic radiation
  • understands light as an electromagnetic phenomenon
