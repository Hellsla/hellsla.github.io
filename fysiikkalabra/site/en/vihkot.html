How to print

Print as a booklet: paper A4, page size A5, double-sided. The printer orders the pages so that the folded sheets make a booklet.

Without a booklet mode, print two pages per sheet double-sided, cut the sheets in half and bind the left edge. The figures are in colour: velocity, acceleration and force are told apart by colour.
