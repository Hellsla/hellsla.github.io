Dronelabra – Fly, program and investigate

Build a programmable drone, learn to fly it safely and investigate by measuring why it stays in the air. Finally, you carry out your own drone project.

More about the courseGoals, contents and assessment.

How a lesson is built

  1. Start right away 5–10 minutes. A first success with a ready-made model, circuit or program.
  2. Try The basic work for everyone, which reaches the lesson's goal.
  3. Modify You change the solution according to your own ideas.
  4. Challenge yourself An optional follow-up task that is harder technically or scientifically.
  5. What did you make? The basic-level output and, if you did the challenge, its output.

The basic level is Start right away, Try and Modify. Challenge yourself and the small challenges in the materials bank are optional. You do not have to do everything in the same lesson.

Goals

Draft: the goals and the lesson outline await the teacher's approval.

In this course, you will learn to

  • fly a drone safely and act as pilot, observer and safety officer
  • explain, using forces and torques, why a quadcopter hovers, tilts and turns
  • assemble a programmable drone and check that it works before a flight
  • program a micro:bit as a radio controller and read the drone's control program
  • plan a flight test, repeat a measurement and assess the sources of error
  • process measurement data with Python and justify your conclusions with a graph
  • plan, build and document your own technical solution

Assessment

The assessment is based on what you produce in the lessons, the project diary, the project and the teacher's observations. The assessment takes into account:

  • safe and responsible conduct in all roles
  • understanding of the programming and the technical implementation
  • systematic experimental work
  • analysis of measurement results and justification of conclusions
  • development based on feedback and measurements
  • documentation of the project
  • teamwork skills and self-assessment

Good flying skill alone does not decide the assessment. A failed prototype can also show excellent skills when the work is systematic, tested and documented with justification. The scope of 2 credits is a preliminary proposal until the course has been approved.
