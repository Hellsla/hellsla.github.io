Lesson 4 · Build and program the Air:bit

An assembled and tested drone

Assembling the drone and function tests

Assembling the frame and motors, and a start-up test without propellers.

In this lesson you learn: You assemble a drone according to the instructions and test safely that it works.

Works with the present equipment

Start right away (5–10 min)

Attach one motor to the frame according to the instructions and show it to the teacher. Carry on only after that.

Assembling the Air:bit 2 and function tests →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Assemble the drone according to the instructions, but leave the propellers off. Start the drone on a level surface and record which letters the micro:bit display shows during start-up.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Do the motor test without propellers and check from your parts map that every motor is in the right place.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Write a troubleshooting card: what do you do if the display reports that the gyroscope or the motor controller cannot be found?

If you have to wait

While you wait for the teacher's check, add to your parts map or help another group read the instructions.

What did you make?

Basic level: An assembled drone that starts up and passes the motor test without propellers.

Challenge yourself: A troubleshooting card for two start-up errors.

Write in your project diary

  • a photo of the assembled drone
  • the start-up stages and the symbols on the display
  • what was hardest about the assembly

Together

Check another group's drone using the checklist before the teacher looks at it.

You need

  • Air:bit 2 building kit
  • micro:bit V2
  • a charged battery
  • a small cross-head screwdriver
  • USB cable

Step-by-step guide

  • Assembling the Air:bit 2 and function tests

Safety

The propellers are not attached in this lesson. The battery is connected only after the teacher has checked the assembly.

  • Drones: propellers and motors
  • Drones: charging and storing batteries

Ahjo's safety instructions are still being prepared. Follow your teacher's induction.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: First motor 10 min, assembly 40 min, start-up and motor test 15 min, summary 10 min. Allow extra time if needed: the manufacturer estimates 2–3 hours for assembly.

Preparation: Charge the batteries. Load the drone program onto the micro:bits in advance or allow time for it. In the manufacturer's instructions the propellers are attached at the end of the assembly chapter: skip that step and keep the propellers yourself until the flying lessons.
