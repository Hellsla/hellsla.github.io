Lesson 16 · Present

Presentation and assessment

In your project now: Presenting the project and assessing your own and the group's work.

A lesson is a guiding model, not an order that binds every group. If your group is at another stage, agree the next step with the teacher and record in the journal what you actually did.

In this lesson you learn: You present the project and assess your own and the group's work.

Next task

Present the project to an audience and make a video of at most one minute. Finally, assess your own and the group's work with the project journal's criteria.
