## Lesson plan (75 min)

- **0–7 min, Think about it.** Read the situation aloud. Pairs think for a minute about what a good answer contains besides the number and note three things. Collect them on the board; do not correct yet.
- **7–17 min, Predict.** The six starting-point questions alone. Check quickly where many answered wrong (usually precision and the slope) and go through them together.
- **17–30 min, Investigate.** Pairs mark answers A and B with the scheme, first separately, then compare. Discuss why B gets more points although its number is wrong, and which step would have revealed the error.
- **30–42 min, Theory summary and example.** Go through the seven steps and how the headings of the model answers match them, the command words and the requirements of a graph. Show with the exam's programs how a formula is written with the formula editor and how a screenshot of a graph is taken. Open the example one stage at a time: before each, ask what has to be written next.
- **42–62 min, Review test in class: the multiple-choice questions.** Everyone does the multiple-choice questions of all eight modules (12 in all, about a minute each) and notes the names of the chapters they got wrong. If there is time left, start the calculation questions from module FY1.
- **62–75 min, Check your understanding and exit ticket.** Three checks alone, the exit ticket orally or on paper.

**At home.** The 12 calculation questions of the review test are solved completely in the notebook following the seven steps (about 3–4 min each) before the next lesson. The long sample problems (Tasks) are done during the course, one at a time, with self-assessment against the marking scheme.

## Using the review test
- The test is diagnostic, not graded. Ask the students to note in their notebook or their own notes the chapter of every question they got wrong; the feedback names it.
- At the start of the next lesson collect, by a show of hands or on an anonymous slip, which modules caused the most difficulty, and give them weight in the review lessons of the course.
- The chapters of FY7 are not on the site yet, so the feedback of the FY7 questions names only the module. Refer students to the textbook or your own material.
- The page does not store answers anywhere; only the students themselves see their results.

## Pair work
- In the Investigate stage one partner marks answer A and the other B, then they swap and compare. Disagreements are settled by the wording of the marking scheme.
- In the long problems a pair can solve the same problem and mark each other's answers before opening the model answer.

## Differentiation
- **Fast students.** Calculation questions already in class; after that the long problem *The pendulum and the free-fall acceleration* with a graphing program and a screenshot in the answer.
- **Slower students.** The Predict questions together with a partner, the multiple-choice questions of four modules of the review test in class and the rest at home, and a shared checklist of the seven steps on display for the whole lesson.

Practise the exam's programs with the practice exams published by the Board.
