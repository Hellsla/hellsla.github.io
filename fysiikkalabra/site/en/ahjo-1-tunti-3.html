Lesson 3 · Design and 3D print

Your own key ring

Basics of 3D modelling

Basic shapes, joining and holes in Tinkercad.

In this lesson you learn: You model an object from basic shapes and save it for printing.

Start right away (5–10 min)

Import the key fob base from the teacher into Tinkercad and write your name on it. Your first model is done.

Basics of 3D modelling →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Finish the key ring and save it as an STL file in the print queue.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Make the shape your own: add your own symbol or pattern and move the hole.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Model something that fixes or improves something in your everyday life, such as a cable holder or a pen stand.

What did you make?

Basic level: Your own modified key ring as an STL file in the print queue.

Challenge yourself: A model of an object that improves something in your everyday life.

Together

Show your unfinished model to the person next to you and ask one question you need help with.

You need

  • computer
  • Tinkercad account
  • key fob base (starter file)

Step-by-step guide

  • Basics of 3D modelling
  • Small extra challenge: Your own stamp

Starter files

  • Key fob base (STL, 50 × 25 × 3 mm) untested draft: the teacher prints or tries it before use

Safety

In this lesson you model on a computer. The teacher or a student inducted on the 3D printer prints the model later.
