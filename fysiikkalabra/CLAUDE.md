# Fysiikkalabra — house rules for every agent

## What this repo is
A digital textbook for Finnish upper-secondary physics (LOPS 2019): theory, simulations and exercises, one folder per lesson under `content/fi/<course>/<lesson-id>/` (its texts; the language-neutral code is in `lessons/<course>/<lesson-id>/`, interface texts in `lang/fi.json`, README "Languages"). `scripts/build.py` renders the lessons whose reviews are current into `site/`, which is committed and published at `https://ruukkilab.fi/` (site/ at the root of the public repository Hellsla/ruukkilabra, pushed by `.github/workflows/ruukkilabra-deploy.yml` after CI passes on main; teacher 2026-11-04). Published pages must never name or link the teacher's other sites (hellsla.github.io); `scripts/deploy_site.py` fails CI if they do. Links inside site/ stay relative. Previews (`build.py <id>` or `--all`) go to the git-ignored `preview/`.

## Courses and scope
- `curriculum.json` is the single source for every course (FY1–FY8): its LOPS 2019 goals and central contents from `docs/lops2019/`, its lesson slots, and optional `scope_notes`, `exercise_levels`, `initialized`. `scripts/courses.py` turns a lesson id into its course, slot and folders; nothing else hardcodes a course.
- Stay inside the course's central contents and scope notes (your packet carries them). Grep the MAOL guide in `docs/lops2019/` when unsure whether something belongs to a course.
- **Lukiokohtaiset opintojaksot** (school-specific courses such as FY10, teacher 2026-10-30) are not in `curriculum.json` and never get national goals: they live in `local-courses.json` (`scripts/local_courses.py`), render to `site/<CODE>.html` and the front page's own section; `docs/PORTAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` is the portal design, `docs/FY10_PLAN.md` the FY10 plan. Experimental investigations (`content/<lang>/tutkimukset/<id>/`, `scripts/investigations.py`) are one canonical page each, reviewed and published like a lesson, and listed by the courses that name them. The course page lists them as a bank with a search and filters (topic, F1–F3, E1–E3, working mode; `investigations.py bank`, `templates/invfilter.js`). **TVT-ohjeet** (`content/<lang>/tvt/<id>/`, `scripts/guides.py`, teacher 2026-10-31) are reusable skill guides (graphs, fitting, slope and its uncertainty, video analysis, spreadsheets, sensors, speed from data, the report), reviewed like an investigation and linked from it (`"guides"` in a tvt prerequisite, `[[ohje:<id>|label]]` in the text), never copied into it. Build later phases one reviewed PR at a time. **Course units** (`content/<lang>/units/<id>/`, `scripts/units.py`, teacher 2026-11-01) are the lessons of a local course written in its own language (FY14 *Road to Fusion*: English, with French; `docs/FY14_PLAN.md`, `docs/fy14/writing-guide.md`): the lesson template and inquiry stages, the course's components in `assets/units/<course>/`, published under the same three-review gate into that language's folder only, listed by chapter on every language's course page.
- **Ahjo Makerspace** (teacher 2026-11-08; `ahjo.json`, `scripts/ahjo.py`, PORTAL_ARCHITECTURE "Ahjo Makerspace"): `ahjo.html`, `ahjo-materiaalit.html`, `ahjo-turvallisuus.html`, `ahjo-1.html`, `ahjo-2.html` in Kiviruukin lukio's colours with the original logo, never redrawn. Ahjo 1 and Ahjo 2 stay `_planned` in `local-courses.json`; a bank resource has one home and is linked, never copied; no laser cutter or CNC; safety topics stay "valmisteilla" until the school's responsible staff approve them.
- `/new-course <FY4>` prepares a course (`scripts/new_course.py`); it never writes goals or slots, which come from the LOPS texts and the teacher.
- **Lessons per course** (teacher's decision 2026-10-26): FY1 and FY2 have 8 lessons each, FY3–FY8 16 each (`planned_lessons` in `curriculum.json`). `/new-course` notes a course whose slots do not match; the missing slots come from the teacher, never from an agent.

## Audience and language
- Students aged 16–19, smart but meeting the idea for the first time. Site language Finnish; agent prompts and reports may be in English.
- Physics terms come from `glossary.json`; add a missing term there, do not invent synonyms.
- **Selkeä suomi** (`content/fi-easy/`, for S2 students; teacher 2026-11-03): the same physics in clearer Finnish, difficult words explained the first time, concrete examples, clear instructions, and 3–5 *Luvun tärkeät käsitteet* in `meta.json` `key_concepts`; NOTES.md §12 is the rule set, `check_i18n.py` checks it.

## Physics and maths conventions
- SI units, upright with a thin space: `9,81 m/s²`. Decimal comma in Finnish text, decimal point in JSON.
- **Every number with its unit is LaTeX, in prose too** (teacher 2026-11-01, "jatkossa näin"; NOTES.md §1): `$10\,\text{N}$`, `$1{,}18\,\text{kN}$`, `$9{,}81\,\text{m/s}^2$`, `$4{,}0\,\%$`, in every language and every text a student reads; only an `aria-label` stays plain. `lint_lesson.py` fails an edited lesson on a plain one; reviewers report one in new or edited text as Major.
- g = 9,81 m/s², always in m/s² (never N/kg). Three significant figures in final answers unless the data has fewer.
- Vectors with an overline in LaTeX (`\overline{F}`, `\overline{F}_{\mu}`; teacher 2026-10-29, never `\mathbf` or `\vec`; `scripts/test_notation.py` checks every text, figure, simulation and interface string in CI), magnitudes plain. Every formula appears once in display LaTeX with its symbols named.
- Every exercise has `answer`, `unit` and `tolerance`; the data carries the significant figures the answer should have, and a student rounding to them stays inside the tolerance. `answers.py` computes every exercise answer; `lesson-answers.py` the numbers of the lesson text and the inquiry.

## Lesson form (human decisions 2026-10-25, every lesson)
1. **Visualise.** `## Ydinajatus` and `## Kaava: <law>` each have at least one inline SVG figure right after the paragraph it explains, and any other theory point with a picture (p = F/A) gets a simple one; every label inside a figure is LaTeX (`foreignObject`, NOTES.md §3, teacher 2026-10-26), and so is every label and value in a simulation's canvas and readout (NOTES.md §1); graphs and figures are of printed-textbook quality (NOTES.md §3 Graphs, Print quality); no paragraph over about 120 words.
2. **Solve symbolically first, then substitute** values with units, in the worked example and every solution; solutions are written in LaTeX (NOTES.md §1, teacher 2026-10-26).
3. **Simulations are visually rich, not slider-rich.** Keep the physics controls; spend the effort on the picture, motion, meaningful colour, labels, a legend. Every animated sim has a speed control (hidas · normaali · nopea) and only labels and values on the canvas; explanations go behind closed boxes under it (Lesson page 4).
4. **Worked examples are staged**: seven stages (rule 7) separated by `<!-- vaihe -->` lines, each with a bold lead-in and one step; the page reveals them one at a time.
5. **Inquiry first, shaped by the topic** (teacher's brief 2026-10-25, `docs/inquiry-spec.md`; 2026-10-26: no fixed template): every lesson has `inquiry.json` (`docs/inquiry.md`) through Pohdinta → Ennusta → Tutki → Teoria → Esimerkit → Tarkista osaamisesi, then Tehtävät (the stages Teoriatiivistelmä and Sovella were renamed Teoria and Esimerkit, teacher 2026-11-05; the keys selita and sovella stay): a phenomenon, a retrieval of prior learning, a committed prediction before the evidence where the topic allows, the evidence that fits the topic (a guided experiment on a simulation when manipulating it reveals something; otherwise a table, a graph, competing explanations or a model to build), the model, a transfer task with hints and common-mistake feedback, three checks. A simulation or a game is there for what it teaches, never for show. Feedback is gated on commitment, never access; no answer is disclosed before the student has acted; theory stays readable on its own.
6. **Chapter names, not ids.** A student never sees a lesson id: "luvussa *Paine ja hydrostaattinen paine*", not "oppitunnilla fy3-4" (teacher 2026-10-26).
7. **A complete model solution for every worked example and every task** (teacher 2026-10-27): a final answer or a formula alone is not enough. Each solution shows (1) what is given, with units, and what is asked; (2) the principle and why it fits the situation; (3) the starting equation and the asked quantity solved in symbols before any number; (4) the unit conversions needed (or that none are); (5) the substitution with units and enough intermediate steps; (6) the answer, rounded to the data's precision, with its unit and why that many figures; (7) a check that the result is physically reasonable. A task answered in words gets a justified model answer; a graph task shows how the graph is read and how the answer follows from it. The worked example opens step by step (its seven stages); an exercise's solution and every `numeric` task's solution sit behind that task's own *Näytä ratkaisu* button, so the student tries first. `meta.solutions` is `all`. Every language version (an S2 version included, when one exists) is as complete: S2 clarifies the language and opens the concepts but drops no step or justification. `scripts/check_solutions.py` (in the lint and CI) reports every missing or incomplete solution; NOTES.md §3 "Complete solutions" says how to write one.
NOTES.md says how to write each; `scripts/lint_lesson.py` and `scripts/test_inquiry.py` check what they can.

## Lesson page (human decisions 2026-10-26, every new or edited lesson)

Side by side only: the illustration beside "Tällä tunnilla opit", a figure beside the paragraph it explains when the two are about as tall (else under it), the simulation beside the experiments (sticky). The worked example's steps run under its problem in the reading column (teacher 2026-11-03).
   Design system (`templates/site.css` header): three levels — the stage (numbered 24 px heading on a full-width rule), the task (one card: heading, situation, question, answers, check, feedback or solution), what sits inside a task (tinted panels); one type scale (title 32–36 px Fraunces, stage 24, task 19, text 18 on a 1.6 line, inside a task 17, interface 15, meta 14 px, Plus Jakarta Sans); the simulations keep 17 px on a 1.65 line (their label metrics depend on it); the stage navigation is the progress bar (teacher 2026-11-03): a sticky chip bar under the top edge at every width (under 700 px one button, "3/7 Tutki", that opens the whole list; teacher 2026-11-10, below), the stage on screen marked, a stage ticked once its required answers are given (kept in localStorage `ruukkilabra:<page>`, `lesson.js` part 2, `test_progress.py`). Fix a layout problem in the shared components, never per lesson.
2. **Pohdinta**, not "Herätä kiinnostus", as the first stage's heading and in the navigation.
3. **Ennusta in a box that starts closed** (`<details>`, keyboard operable); it can be reopened later, and closing it keeps the answers and reasons.
4. **Little text in the simulation.** The picture, the controls and the key quantities and values show; usage, the model's assumptions and the computation sit behind closed boxes named Käyttöohje, Mallin oletukset and Laskennan selitys (the sim activity's `howto`, `model`, `calc` in inquiry.json; its visible `text` is a lead of at most 30 words). Never hide a control the student needs; sim.js shows at most one legend line.
5. **Teoria**, not "Selitä" or "Teoriatiivistelmä", as the theory stage's heading and in the navigation; the stage after it is **Esimerkit** (the worked example, then practice with hints), not "Sovella", which names only the module's *Sovella itsenäisesti* (teacher 2026-11-05). Esimerkit and Tarkista osaamisesi open with one line on what they are for (lang `inquiry.lead_<stage>`). Since 2026-11-06 (teacher): the last stage is **Itsearvio** (was Tarkista osaamisesi; id `tarkista`), its exit ticket **Pohdintatehtävä** and its checks named by kind, *Käsitetehtävä*, *Laskutehtävä*, *Selitystehtävä* (`inquiry.task_titles`); every box of Esimerkit is a guided example, "Esimerkki 1: <name>", the worked example's short name in `example-names.json` (outside the lessons, so no review goes stale; a new lesson adds its name there); Palaa ennusteisiisi and Kertaa luvun käsitteet are closed boxes. Older texts that say Teoriatiivistelmä / Theory summary are shown with the new name (`inquiry.current_stage_names`), so their reviews stand; new text writes Teoria / Theory.
6. **The formula section is named after its law**: `## Kaava: Lämpömäärä` shows "Lämpömäärä" (or Hydrostaattinen paine, Ideaalikaasun tilanyhtälö, by the lesson's content); "Kaava" stays the key that inquiry.json and the scripts use.
7. **Exit ticket**, not "Lähtölippu", in every heading and link a student sees. It opens Tarkista osaamisesi, before the checks, and has no step of its own in the navigation (teacher 2026-11-04, "yksi vaihe vähemmän"); its lead is the shared `inquiry.exit_lead`.
8. **Tehtävät last**: the exercises close the page, after Tarkista osaamisesi, with their levels, answer checks and solutions; predictions and the questions of the investigation stay where they are.
9. **Textbook illustrations**: every chapter opens with an illustration of its Miksi question (teacher 2026-10-29, NOTES.md §3 "Opening illustration", checked by the lint); polished inline SVG in the figure palette (NOTES.md §3), recognisable materials, consistent strokes, room around things, labels that read on a phone; colour carries the physics (a temperature rise stands out) but never alone; quantities to scale. `content/fi/FY3/fy3-6` is the model.

## Readability (teacher 2026-10-27, every lesson; NOTES.md §8)
The teacher must never again have to point out a text wall, a stray figure, cramped formulas or an empty area left by an opened task. The shared templates do the layout; the writer does the text:
- **Text**: one main idea per paragraph (about 120 words at most), short content-naming `###` subheadings where a section holds several ideas, bold only for a key definition or conclusion, no extra coloured boxes. Never remove a physical reason or causal step to shorten text: restructure instead.
- **Opening**: `learn` in inquiry.json holds 2–3 one-line goals from the student's side, shown as *Tällä tunnilla opit*, so each goal completes that sentence (teacher 2026-10-29): a lowercase -maan/-mään verb, parallel when it joins skills ("laskemaan …", "vertailemaan … ja selittämään …"; English "In this lesson, you will learn to" + "calculate …"); never "Osaat …" there. Module goals ("Opiskelija osaa …"), *Pohjatiedot* and rubrics keep their own grammar. `scripts/objectives.py` checks it (errors in the validator, suspicious forms as lint warnings), and a goal rephrased with its symbols and numbers unchanged is the review category `objective-form` (science none, pedagogy delta); `prior` is a list (*Pohjatiedot*), each item ending "(luku *<chapter>*)"; one Miksi question (or two short sentences).
- **Figures** right after the paragraph, table or list they explain, never apart from it; the build decides beside or under; no figure after a long block it does not explain.
- **Calculations** as one inline chain per step in the source; the page shows long ones as aligned rows that wrap on a phone, the answer set apart and the reasonableness check as its own block (`scripts/mathlayout.py`). Never split a chain by hand for the layout.
- **Tasks**: the situation first, the question last as its own sentence; feedback, hints and solutions open inside the task's card.
- **Checks**: `check_layout.py` (in `check_lesson.py` and CI) fails a page with overflow, overlapping blocks, cards out of reading order, a large empty column beside an opened card or a figure, over-long lines, text under 17 px or controls under 40 px, or a heading under the sticky navigation, in every open state; fix the shared component and add a check when a new kind of problem appears (NOTES.md §6). `screenshot.py <id>` gives the captures of the opened states for a look before review.

## Exercise levels
Level 1 (perustehtävät): one formula or one step of reasoning, values given. Level 2 (soveltavat): two steps, a conversion, a rearrangement, or measured data to work with. Level 3 (haastavat): reasoning, two ideas, modelling, data interpretation or an unfamiliar context, in the spirit of the YO exam's problem solving. None repeats the worked example. The counts are minimums (`curriculum.json` `exercise_levels`, default 5/4/3, and `exercise_total`): **the exercise bank** (teacher 2026-11-04) gives every FY1–FY8 chapter at least 20 exercises, about 8 + 7 + 5 (the topic may shift it), more where extra tasks are pedagogically justified; a module's `exercise_total` is set to 20 when all its chapters have them. Existing exercises, their ids and solutions are kept: new ones are added beside them with the next free ids. Beside the calculations, which stay plentiful, the bank mixes conceptual multiple choice (`"choices"`), justified qualitative answers, experimental design, error analysis and graph reading, and 2–3 **aineistotehtävää** in most chapters, mostly among the level-2 tasks (a table of measurements, `"data"`, or a graph by its figure id: read, fit, judge the model or the uncertainty). `"kind"` names what a task practises (`courses.EXERCISE_KINDS`); `python scripts/exercise_bank.py <COURSE>` reports each chapter's counts and mix; the plan per chapter is `docs/exercise-bank/`.

## Exam readiness (teacher's brief 2026-10-28, NOTES.md §9; a guiding principle for every course)
- **Inquiry stays the foundation.** The stages keep their purpose; exam-style questions are not spread into every stage. Progression is visible at the module level: understanding → guided application (Sovella, exercises 1–2) → independent application (level 3, a transfer task without hints) → exam-style transfer (Sovella itsenäisesti).
- **Self-assessment** (teacher 2026-10-28): no chapter-level "Osaan / Tarvitsen vielä harjoitusta" checklist. Written answers are self-assessed against the model answer's observable criteria, then "what is missing" and where to practise (NOTES.md §11); multiple-choice checks are graded by the page; the point-based rubric stays in Sovella itsenäisesti.
- **Figures** (NOTES.md §11): find an existing figure in `docs/figures/catalog.json` (`python scripts/figcat.py find …`) before drawing one; a task shows a figure by its id (`"figure": "fy1-4/scatter-points"`), never a pasted SVG; a variant without the answer is drawn from the same data in the owner's figures.py.
- **Sovella itsenäisesti**: each course ends with original module-end tasks, as many as its goals need (teacher 2026-10-29: evaluate the goals, never three by default; FY1 2, FY2 1, FY3 3), written once per course with `/practice <COURSE>` (`content/<lang>/<COURSE>/sovella/`, README "Sovella itsenäisesti"): authentic situation and source material, parts with points, nothing that names the model, then behind one button a complete worked solution, common mistakes and the shared rubric for self-assessment. Never relabel chapter exercises; prefer replacing a weak activity over adding one.
- **Esimerkkikoe** (teacher 2026-11-05): after a course is complete, `/exam <COURSE>` writes its example exam from `docs/exam-guidelines.md` (README "Esimerkkikoe"). Teacher-written reference exams are private: never in the repository, a packet, a review, a commit or memory, and never paraphrased.
- **Competencies** (`competencies.json`): concept, math, graph, experiment, communication, transfer. Consider them for every course, never as quotas, never off-curriculum. `scripts/coverage.py <COURSE>` writes the evidence-based report `docs/coverage/<COURSE>.md`; a gap is closed by a better activity, not by tagging.
- **Token economy**: generate once, reuse. Shared rubric criteria by key (texts once in `lang/<lang>.json`), one rubric component, deterministic checks (`practice.py check`, `test_practice.py`, `coverage.py --check`) before any review, no new agents, no model at runtime, translate only new or changed text.

## Pipeline
- `/practice <COURSE>` (`.claude/commands/practice.md`) writes and reviews a course's Sovella itsenäisesti tasks from `practice.py packet` files; their reviews are in `content/fi/<COURSE>/sovella/review/` and gate `site/<COURSE>-sovella.html` like a lesson's.
- **developer** writes and fixes, **science-reviewer**, **pedagogy-reviewer** and **visual-editorial-reviewer** (teacher 2026-10-31: figures, graphs, mathematical typography, Finnish, presentation, judged from the rendered captures) judge independently; each works from a packet file (`scripts/review_packet.py`) that its prompt names. `docs/EDITORIAL_QUALITY_STANDARD.md` is the shared yardstick (ten dimensions, 1–5 rubrics, Critical/Major/Minor); `/quality-audit <id> --audit-only|--fix` (`.claude/commands/quality-audit.md`) audits existing content of any type and corrects it through the same gate. `/lesson <id>` (`.claude/commands/lesson.md` at the repository root, beside `.claude/agents/`: routing, budgets, steps) runs a round; `/exercises <id> <task>` (`.claude/commands/exercises.md`) works on a lesson's exercises alone, from an exercise packet that carries the lesson's formulas and worked example (the exercise brief) instead of the lesson; `python scripts/check_lesson.py <id>` runs every deterministic check before any review.
- **Physics accuracy** (teacher 2026-10-28, after the FY1–FY3 audit): `docs/physics-guardrails.md` is the short physics reference every developer and science-reviewer packet carries (findings A error, B unstated condition, C fair simplification; one explanation worded the same everywhere it appears). `scripts/physics_phrases.py` warns in the lint about known wrong claims taught as fact and `test_physics_phrases.py` keeps them out in CI; `scripts/concepts.py` gathers one concept's sentences from every chapter for a cross-chapter science review when a change touches a concept in several chapters. The audit record is `docs/audits/fy1-fy3-physics-audit.md`.
- **The rule** (`scripts/reviews.py`): a lesson is on the site when all three reviews say PASS for its current content hash. Content published before the visual reviewer existed stays on the site under the hash-bound **visual baseline** (`docs/visual-baseline.tsv`, never added to by hand) until its first material change, which then needs a genuine visual PASS; `reviews.py baseline` lists what still awaits that review. Its exercise set has its own hash and its own three reviews (`exercises/review/`, `--exercises`): the page shows the version they last passed, so new exercises never take the lesson off the site. The hash covers the lesson files, its simulation folder, its pictures and its simulation tests; formatting, build scripts, templates and configuration never stale a review. What changed decides who looks again (`reviews.py scope`: none, delta or full per reviewer); a PASS that holds over a change its reviewer need not see is recorded in `review/carry.tsv` (`reviews.py carry`) and re-derived by CI. **Figures the teacher approved** (teacher 2026-11-10: "kuvat olen itse hyväksynyt"; new ones are checked for layout only) need no science, pedagogy or visual review: after the teacher has seen them, `reviews.py approve-figures <id>` records each figure's exact Finnish drawing in `docs/teacher-figures.tsv`, and the change (the drawing, its words in a translation, a task given it by `"figure"`) is the category `teacher-figure`; `check_layout.py` checks the pages. Any later change to the drawing or its shared style needs a new approval. There are no status fields and nothing to edit by hand. `review/legacy.md` marks a prototype lesson published before this pipeline until any content change retires it.
- Merging the pull request is the teacher's publication decision. CI rebuilds `site/` and fails if the committed copy differs; a lesson whose reviews went stale is left out with a WARNING, never silently.
- **CI tests only the changed pages** (teacher 2026-11-01, "testaa vain muuttuneet sivut"): on a pull request the three browser steps (`test_sims.py`, the per-lesson inquiry tests, `check_layout.py` at eight widths: about 1 h 30 min of a full run) test only the pages the change can alter, as `scripts/ci_scope.py` names them (a lesson's, investigation's, guide's or Sovella set's own files in any language, its simulation tests, the assets it uses, its sim texts in `lang/`, and every page whose committed `site/` HTML changed, so a neighbour whose chapter links changed is tested too). A change to anything shared (`scripts/`, `templates/`, shared `site/` files, `curriculum.json` and the other configuration, other `lang/` texts, `docs/visual-baseline.tsv`, the workflow) runs them in full, and so do main, the weekly run and a manual run (Actions → fysiikkalabra-ci → Run workflow). All other checks always run in full. Keep a pull request to one module or a few chapters so its run stays short; before pushing, run the same browser checks locally for exactly the pages `python scripts/ci_scope.py origin/main` prints. A new kind of file that changes a page needs its row in `ci_scope.py` (unknown files mean a full run, never a skipped test). A newer push to a pull request cancels its older run.
