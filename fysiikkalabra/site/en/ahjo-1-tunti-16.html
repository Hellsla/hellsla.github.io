Lesson 16 · Your own project

A small exhibition

Presentation and self-assessment

Finishing, presenting the mini project and assessing your own learning.

In this lesson you learn: You present your work and assess your own learning.

Start right away (5–10 min)

Film a 30-second video of your device working.

Project journal →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Present your device: the goal, the solution and the result.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Choose your own way to present: a demo, a poster, a video or an exhibition stand.

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Say what you would do in the next version, and assess your learning with the project journal's criteria.

What did you make?

Basic level: A presentation and a completed project journal.

Challenge yourself: A plan for the next version and a self-assessment with the project journal's criteria.

Together

Try two other people's devices and leave each one a compliment and one idea for improvement.

You need

  • the finished device
  • phone to film a video
  • project diary

Step-by-step guide

Safety

At the show your device is close to the audience. Check that it has no hot parts, sharp edges or loose wires.
