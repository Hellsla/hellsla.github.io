Physics beyond the textbook

Book tips

Physics is not only formulas and calculations. It is also stories, surprising questions, experiments and attempts to understand the world.

In these books you can find new views of familiar phenomena, or meet physics there is no time for in lessons. You do not have to read a book from cover to cover. You can start with one chapter that interests you.

If physics interests you, these books are worth a look. They cover the history of physics, everyday phenomena, thought experiments and some rather unusual questions.

Borrow from Lauri The first nine books on the list are on my own shelf, so feel free to ask to borrow them!

  1. Herra Tompkins fysiikan ihmemaassa

    George Gamow

    Borrow from Lauri

    What would happen if the speed of light were as slow as the speed of cars? Imaginative adventures make the strange effects of relativity and quantum physics understandable.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY4, FY8

  2. Kuolleiden kanssa kassajonossa – Tieteellistä aikamatkailua

    Leena Tähtinen

    Borrow from Lauri

    Could you travel into the past? Or into the future? The book explores time and the possibilities of time travel from the point of view of physics.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8

  3. Entäs jos… / What If?

    Randall Munroe

    Borrow from Lauri

    What would happen if everyone in the world jumped at the same time? Absurd questions lead to surprisingly serious physics and fun calculations.

    Language
    Finnish / English
    Level
    easy
    Especially for
    FY1–FY4

  4. What If? 2: Additional Serious Scientific Answers to Absurd Hypothetical Questions

    Randall Munroe

    Borrow from Lauri

    More imaginative questions with scientific answers. Could you cool the atmosphere by opening every freezer at once? The book shows how physics can be used to investigate almost any question.

    Language
    English
    Level
    easy
    Especially for
    FY1–FY4

  5. Seitsemän lyhyttä luentoa fysiikasta

    Carlo Rovelli

    Borrow from Lauri

    A short, thought-provoking book on the big ideas of modern physics: relativity, quantum physics and the universe.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8

  6. The Physics of Superheroes

    James Kakalios

    Borrow from Lauri

    Could Spider-Man really swing from his web? How fast could the Flash run? And how do Superman's powers square with Newton's laws? The book examines superheroes' abilities with real physics.

    Language
    English
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY2–FY5, FY7

  7. Ajan lyhyt historia

    Stephen Hawking

    Borrow from Lauri

    Where did the universe come from? What are black holes? Does time have a beginning or an end? A well-known classic of modern cosmology.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    intermediate–demanding
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8

  8. Pimeän aineen arvoitus

    Tommi Tenkanen

    Borrow from Lauri

    Most of the matter in the universe is invisible. How do we know it exists, and what could it be?

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8

  9. Thirty Years That Shook Physics: The Story of Quantum Theory

    George Gamow

    Borrow from Lauri

    The quantum revolution of the first decades of the 20th century, told by a physicist of that era. Planck, Bohr, Heisenberg, Schrödinger and the other founders of the theory all appear.

    Language
    English
    Level
    demanding
    Especially for
    FY8, FY14

  10. Storm in a Teacup: The Physics of Everyday Life

    Helen Czerski

    Why does popcorn pop? Why does drying coffee leave a ring? Everyday phenomena open connections to the big principles of physics.

    Language
    English
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1–FY4

  11. Laskette varmaankin leikkiä, Mr Feynman!

    Richard P. Feynman

    Humorous and sometimes astonishing memoirs of a Nobel-winning physicist. Above all, the book is about curiosity, experimenting and the way a scientist thinks.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    easy
    Especially for
    FY1, FY10

  12. Fysiikan sankareita viideltä vuosisadalta

    Heikki Oja

    Stories of the people who shaped physics, from Pascal to Higgs. A good choice if you want to know how ideas in physics have changed.

    Language
    Finnish
    Level
    easy–intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY8, FY14

  13. The Ten Most Beautiful Experiments

    George Johnson

    Ten landmark experiments in the history of science. The book shows how a simple but cleverly designed experiment can lead to a great scientific discovery.

    Language
    English
    Level
    intermediate
    Especially for
    FY1, FY10

  14. The Flying Circus of Physics

    Jearl Walker

    A collection of strange, fun and sometimes dangerous-looking phenomena of physics. The book invites you to ask questions and look for explanations.

    Language
    English
    Level
    intermediate
    Especially for
    FY3–FY5, FY10
