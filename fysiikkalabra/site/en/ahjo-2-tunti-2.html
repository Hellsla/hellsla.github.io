Lesson 2 · Invent

Defining the problem

In your project now: Whose problem, what need, and how success is recognised. Stage 2: the user's needs.

A lesson is a guiding model, not an order that binds every group. If your group is at another stage, agree the next step with the teacher and record in the journal what you actually did.

In this lesson you learn: You find out what the user needs and define a measure of success.

Next task

Find out what users need. Interview at least three users, or if it suits your project better, observe the situation of use or gather the need in another reasoned way, for example with a survey. In an artistic project the user can be a viewer or a listener. Write the problem as: how might we help a user group in a situation where … Add a measure of success: a number you can measure or a clear qualitative sign.
