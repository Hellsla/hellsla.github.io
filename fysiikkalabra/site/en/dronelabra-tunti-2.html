Lesson 2 · Get to know drones and fly safely

Force diagram of hovering

Why does a drone fly? Forces and torques

Weight, lift and the directions in which the propellers spin: how a quadcopter climbs, tilts and turns.

In this lesson you learn: You explain hovering as a balance of forces and tilting using torques.

Works with the present equipment

Start right away (5–10 min)

Cut a helicopter out of paper following the teacher's template and drop it three times. Measure the fall time with a stopwatch.

Free-body diagrams and the combined effect of forces →

Try · Modify · Challenge yourself

  1. Try for everyone

    Draw the force diagram of a hovering drone and calculate how much lift one propeller has to produce when you know the mass of the drone. Mark the directions in which the propellers spin on the diagram.

  2. Modify your own ideas

    Change the blade length of the paper helicopter and measure the fall time with five drops. How does the blade length affect it?

  3. Challenge yourself optional

    Explain, using forces and torques, why the drone turns about its vertical axis when two opposite propellers spin faster than the others.

What did you make?

Basic level: A force diagram of a hovering drone and the calculated lift of one propeller.

Challenge yourself: An explanation of how the drone turns, using torques.

Write in your project diary

  • the force diagram and the calculation
  • the measurement results for the paper helicopter
  • which result differed from what you expected, and why

Together

Compare your force diagram with your partner's. Are the lengths of the forces in the right proportion?

You need

  • paper and scissors
  • a stopwatch or a phone
  • a ruler
  • a calculator
  • the mass of the drone (from the teacher or a scale)

Step-by-step guide

  • Free-body diagrams and the combined effect of forces
  • Moment: the turning effect of a force

Safety

There is no flying in this lesson. The drones stay in their boxes.

For the teacher: preparation and timing

Timing: Paper helicopter 15 min, theory and force diagram 25 min, measurements 25 min, summary 10 min.

Preparation: Print the paper helicopter template and weigh one drone in advance. If necessary, revise Newton's laws and torque.
